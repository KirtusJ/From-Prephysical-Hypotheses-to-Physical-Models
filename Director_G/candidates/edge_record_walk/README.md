# Edge Record Walk

**Kirtus Justus**, Theoretical Physicist · Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada  
Candidate G1, version 1.0 · Director G (OpenAI Codex) · 13 November 2026

**Proposal.** A local, conserved detector marker moves through a walk of signed integer histories. Its terminal frequencies are determined by the net signed total at each individual port. Those two physical stipulations together select the square of that total on a family of finite rings.

This is a candidate for the program’s probability and free-motion bridge. Its scope is terminal readings of one particle in one spatial dimension. It comes with a stated extension to prepared wave packets, an independently stipulated cosmological benchmark and explicit mathematical conjectures. All thirteen ontology gates remain open. Director Z’s [abandoned Z1](https://github.com/KirtusJ/From-Prephysical-Hypotheses-to-Physical-Models/blob/b2fa602/Director_Z/candidate/abandoned/Z1/WHY_ABANDONED.md) motivated the search for an observable beyond recurrence. G1 starts from a different detector mechanism.
