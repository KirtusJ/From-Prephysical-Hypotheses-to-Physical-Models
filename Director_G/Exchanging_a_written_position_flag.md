# Testing a walk position by exchanging two unread registers

**Kirtus Justus**, Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada. Research supplement by Director G (OpenAI Codex). Codex workers contributed the signed-flight construction, the criterion for splitting a recorded bin, the detector counterexamples and independent checks.

After three signed Hadamard steps from a sharp right-moving source, the well at \(+1\) carries squared mass \(5/8\). A spatial detector can disagree even if its reference measurements are perfectly calibrated. Here the apparatus writes the position answer into a physical bit **before** doing anything to compare it with a reference. It then operates exclusively on other coordinates. Two programs finish with the same full amplitude table. Comparing their actual bit frequencies tests the transfer of a probability calibration to this position. A four-setting extension covers identifiable finite signed columns whose individual recorded bins cannot be split in this way. The predictions have not been measured.

## The same bit after two preparations

The finite walk has signed integer amplitudes \(n_i/\sqrt S\) on position, rail and any retained records, with \(S=\sum_i n_i^2=2^t\). Start with a known coherent column. Write only \(B(i)\), the membership of a specified bin, into a blank bit \(F\). The desired squared mass is \(p=P/S\), where \(P=\sum_{B(i)=1}n_i^2\). No outcome probability has entered this calculation.

Suppose the amplitudes inside each nonempty bin can be transformed into positive unit numerators. The allowed operations are interchanges, sign changes, clean coordinates and addressed four-mode Walsh gates. They act on the *carrier*, in two predetermined blocks according to the original bin membership, regardless of the contents of \(F\). For these finite known columns the necessary and sufficient condition is that the odd part of the gcd of the numerators inside each nonempty bin be one. The constructive proof reduces a row to entries of the same magnitude, its odd gcd. Finish with a reversible permutation that names the resulting \(S\) occupied cells \(J=0,\ldots,S-1\), with the \(P\) marked cells first. Other accessible workspace must have a common final state. Conditioning later gates on a separate surviving copy of the original site can prevent this equality. [Construction, resource bound and odd-divisor obstruction](Stable_flattening_inside_a_recorded_bin.md)

Give the same experiment a second \(S\)-cell register \(K\) with equal **amplitudes**, initially independent in the complete state. A sequence of the walk’s returned clean bits can supply this register from the incident carrier. Use \(M(j)=\mathbf1\{j<P\}\). Two programs then perform exactly the same carrier transformations. In program T the first write is \(F=B(i)\). In program R the first write is \(F=M(K)\). In the ordering \(J,K,F\) their final columns are
\[
 T=\frac1S\sum_{j,k}|j,k,M(j)\rangle,\qquad
 R=\frac1S\sum_{j,k}|j,k,M(k)\rangle .
\]
Interchanging \(J\) and \(K\) maps \(T\) to \(R\) without touching the already-written \(F\). The old position, the helper that supplied the reference and possible route labels are part of this test: if any still distinguishes the programs, the displayed columns alone are incomplete. The exchange is a circuit on accessible coordinates and can entail transport, delays and control of two registers of \(S\) addresses. Its full product table has \(S^2\) address pairs. An individually sharp launch at a different unknown rail or with an inaccessible entangled source record is a distinct preparation. [Returned-bit reference and independent-qubit control](Probability_reference_after_a_position_record.md)

Here is one way to bound the physical inference. Measure the target rate on the original position write, on its matched realization just described after the carrier operations and exchange, and on program R at the same terminal bit. Let the first change in rate be bounded by \(e_{\rm hold}\), including the effect of adding the unused reference and the elapsed clock. Let the difference of the latter two rates be bounded by \(e_{\rm match}\). Calibrate the reference side on the same loaded apparatus to within \(e_{\rm ref}\) of its known fraction. Then the original spatial rate is within \(e_{\rm hold}+e_{\rm match}+e_{\rm ref}\) of \(p\). Sharply reading a bit and restarting before the exchange changes the experiment. These are comparisons on original launches with a specified detector, coherent write and isolation of its output. Equality of endpoint vectors is a reason to test the match. It does not establish that a physical history-sensitive detector passes it. Envariance gives an earlier approach using exchanges of entangled subsystems and equal branches. Exact dyadic synthesis describes the available finite gates. [Zurek](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0405161) · [Li, Ross and Selinger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.01175)

For more general joint input amplitudes $a(j,k,e)$, where $e$ names all untouched spectator coordinates, the two flagged columns match exactly with the stated phase convention if and only if $a(j,k,e)=a(k,j,e)$ for every triple. Evaluate the swapped column and the reference column at $F=M(k)$ to recover that condition. A common global phase can be fixed when comparing rays. The test concerns the complete preparation even when a reduced bit has the same density matrix. [Lean statement and error algebra](lean/FlagExchange.lean)

**A reference count without assuming uniform output probabilities.** On reference diagnostic shots, choose an independent cyclic shift \(a\) uniformly in \(\mathbb Z/S\), or use the equivalent stratification with comparable incidents. Write \(M(K-a)\) at the same clock. On a common complete address read with distribution \(\pi(k)\), the decoded rate averaged over all shifts is exactly \(P/S\): every \(k\) belongs to \(P\) of the translated masks. No shape assumption on \(\pi\) enters. Test that each actual coherent mask write agrees with that decoded read or bounds its discrepancy, and that translations of the unread uniform reference after a write agree with the corresponding shifted-write preparations. Their complete columns coincide after relabeling. If the decoded address population changes with the mask, or copying an address before applying the mask introduces extra records, these comparisons are additional experimental obligations. At large \(S\) a full menu of \(S\) translations costs settings as well as memory. A separately measured reference on the loaded apparatus can be used instead.

## A spatial example and three failed controls

At tick three the five occupied coordinates \((-3,L),(-1,R),(1,L),(1,R),(3,R)\) have signed numerators \(1,-1,1,2,1\). Flip the phase at \((-1,R)\). Split the \(2\) at \((1,R)\) into four 1s using one four-mode Walsh on that coordinate and three blank modes. The remaining entries are already units. Five of the eight resulting addresses came from \(x=+1\). Both programs use a complete permutation of the 128 physical position/rail/scratch addresses in the checker. Exchanging the two three-bit registers matches all 64 final nonzero amplitudes at \(1/8\).

Retaining an inaccessible exact copy of the old \(J\) in both programs gives squared vector difference \(7/4\) under the exchange. An uncompensated sign on one address gives \(7/8\), and one amplitude doubled before normalization gives \(7/44\). These are circuit-level differences, not probabilities of detecting the fault. A detector rival changes original history weights by a factor three when \(B=1\) while leaving independent reference labels ordinary. It gives the target rate \(3p/(1+2p)=5/6\) and the reference rate \(5/8\). Its gap is \(5/24\). Boolean recombination and a fair XOR can still look correct under that weighted history law. ## When an odd divisor prevents the comparison

At four ticks, the single rail \((+2,R)\) has numerator 3 and squared mass \(9/16\). Protecting its membership during deterministic dyadic operations leaves every marked numerator divisible by three over the dyadic rationals. Tensoring an independent clean dyadic reference after the write preserves this obstruction. The whole site \(+2\), in contrast, also includes numerator 1. Its two rails can split into ten unit cells. [Exact four-tick comparison](Stable_flattening_inside_a_recorded_bin.md)

For **any known column** of the stipulated form, prepare two clean equal-amplitude helper bits \(L\) before writing \(F\). In one of the four helper values write 1 for every source coordinate, in a second write 0 for every source coordinate, and in the other two write the actual \(B(i)\). Perform these writes coherently, reading neither \(L\) nor the original \(B\). The new marked and unmarked squared numerators are \(S+2P\) and \(3S-2P\), with total \(4S\). In each block a complete copy of the original integer column appears. Any common odd divisor of the original entries would have its square dividing \(2^t\), so both blocks have odd gcd one. Consequently each block admits a deterministic split, and the preceding exchange calibrates the resulting flag at
\[
 q=\frac14+\frac12p.
\]
There is no postselection or failure branch in this construction. The helper changes the written predicate; recovering the rate for the original position write requires a further comparison.

On separate incidents choose the same four settings *classically*, with fractions \(1/4,1/4,1/2\) for the certain-hit, certain-miss and true-bin arms. Use documented independent assignments or valid stratified averages. Compare their aggregate rate with the coherent helper’s measured rate, allowing mismatch \(e_c\). Let \(e_q\) bound the coherent rate’s discrepancy from \(q\) through the loaded reference experiment, \(e_b\) bound the change between the honest copy and the original bin rate \(r\), and \(\eta_1,\eta_0\) bound the certain-hit failure and certain-miss false hit. The two endpoint errors have opposite signs. Ordinary accounting on these separately assigned trials gives
\[
 |r-p|\le 2(e_c+e_q)+\tfrac12\max(\eta_1,\eta_0)+e_b .
\]
The inequality uses no smoothness or scalar response curve for the meter. Each transfer tolerance needs its measured bound. Keep incomplete bits in each incident denominator, bounding hits by counting every unresolved bit as either 0 or 1; settings that physically change the source or create a new outcome need their own model.

For the \(9/16\) rail the coherent helper predicts \(17/32\). The factor-three history rival gives \(27/34\) on the original rail and \(11/17\) on the classical mixture, a difference of \(63/544\) from the coherent squared-mass prediction. The native gates and common reference in the checker produce 34 positive unit cells in the marked block and 30 in the other. It propagates the two resulting 4,096-entry columns explicitly. The recorded example of 66 hits and three unresolved bits in 128 launches is a hypothetical counting example only. [Exact circuits and interval checks](results/flag_exchange.json)

## Worked scope of the bridge

One finite known synchronized signed flight, including a *reversibly written record word*, has precisely this integer-column form when the other controls are signed permutations, coordinate writes and common-depth balanced ticks from a localized source. Apply the procedure to an acceptance word and to its intersection with a whole-path predicate. To transfer to conditioned paths the rate errors must be small relative to the acceptance yield, and the retained word must represent the actual read. For a prepared Newtonian trajectory there is also a cheaper terminal test of the two candidate tracks using a detector reliable near certainty. Its relative track weights remain open. Circuit size for the present equal-amplitude method can be exponential in flight depth. The development gives a finite comparison for a spatial probability; no experimental frequencies, identification of physical time or absolute cyclotomic label follow from the circuit.
