# Selecting a cyclic phase with a real two-coordinate reference

**Kirtus Justus**, Theoretical Physicist  
Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada

Research supplement by Director G (OpenAI Codex).

The walk can prepare a relation between the fundamental character of a held finite cyclic translation and the orientation of a specified real two-coordinate plane. Both start in localized coordinates. The rational reference operation used to select them can be compiled from equal-depth signed gates. Once prepared, the same two coordinates read the sine as well as the cosine of the chosen stroke. This replaces the earlier exhaustive stroke menu for this task with repeated comparisons to one constructed rotation. It requires a different apparatus: coherent control of the stroke, a real signed quarter-turn, and records that distinguish the comparison outcomes without identifying the arm. ## Constructing the reference

On the real plane choose the oriented signed permutation $J(u,v)=(-v,u)$, with $J^2=-I$. Take a rational rotation $R=(aI+bJ)/c$, $a^2+b^2=c^2$, $c>0$. The [synchronized rational circuit](Rational_references_and_optical_bypass.md) implements $R\oplus I$ using $|a|$ signed identity labels and $|b|$ signed $J$ labels on the acted arm, and $c$ identity labels on the idle arm. Pad to dyadic $K\ge |a|+|b|$ and apply the Walsh preparation, signed selection, inverse Walsh and coarse herald. The successful operator is $(c/K)(R\oplus I)$ with $p=c^2/K^2>1/8$ for least dyadic padding. On retry use the actual adjoint, including $J^\dagger=-J$. Success is almost sure with mean $1+1/p<9$ calls for $p<1$; $p=1$ completes on the first call. A finite deadline in calls retains the full timeout probability. This is an ideal apparatus with known high-dimensional mode addressing. [Physical numerator and adjoint checks](check_rational_phase_comparison.py)

Let $q\ge5$ be the known order of the tested physical stroke $T$. Its target angle $\alpha=2\pi/q$ is in the first quadrant. One can choose $R$ by integer calculations. Enumerate first-quadrant Pythagorean points using $(a,b,c)\propto(D^2-n^2,2Dn,D^2+n^2)$ for integers $0<n<D$. Track exact powers of $a+ib$ through one set of $q$ strokes, counting crossings of the positive real ray. Require the endpoint to be in the right half-plane and the nearest winding number to be one. For $\phi=\arg(a+ib)$ this gives $|q\phi-2\pi|<\pi/2$, and

$$|\phi-\alpha|\le \frac{2|\mathrm{Im}(a+ib)^q|}{q c^q}.$$

The right side uses only integers. The rational parametrization is dense in the quadrant, so arbitrarily small positive tolerances can be attained for each fixed $q$. At $q=5$, denominator grid 128 gave $(7735,23808,25033)$ and certified an error below $0.000053$ radians. The unit of the physical stroke, the orientation of $J$ and its coordinate metric enter this definition. [Integer winding checks at four orders](results/rational_phase_comparison.json)

## Measuring and retaining the relation

Prepare one sheet site, one plane basis vector and a separate clean real comparison rail. Apply $H$, translate the sheet on one arm and rotate the plane by $R$ on the other, apply $H$ again and record plus or minus. The two effective operators on the common data are $(T\otimes I\pm I\otimes R)/2$. A rational synthesis herald for $R\oplus I$ is internal to each trial, using the same successful scalar on both arms. It must be resolved or recovered before accepting an interference outcome. Fix a Fourier convention in which the eigenvalues of $T$ and $J$ are $e^{2\pi i k/q}$ and $si$, $s=\pm1$. A plus click has sector probability

$$p_{ks}=\frac{1+\cos(k\alpha-s\phi)}2.$$

The real localized product input has joint weight $1/(2q)$ in every eigenspace. Its first bright probability is $1/2$, irrespective of $R$. Two successive bright records have joint probability $3/8$ at these orders. Replacing both registers between comparisons gives $1/4$. The ability to keep the *same* sheet and plane is observable.

For $\delta=|\phi-\alpha|<\pi/q$ the uniquely maximizing pair of joint labels is $(k,s)=(1,+1),(-1,-1)$. Write $G=\cos^2(\delta/2)$ and $B=\cos^2((\alpha-\delta)/2)$. After $r$ successive bright outcomes on the retained data, the joint probability of those outcomes and the correct relation is $G^r/q$. The posterior odds for all other labels combined are at most $(q-1)(B/G)^r$. For a different prior replace $1/q$ by its weight on the correct subspace. A prior with none there cannot acquire it by this filter. A fixed imperfect angle has $G<1$, so taking $r\to\infty$ at fixed precision costs all yield. A family with $\delta=O(1/r)$ at fixed $q$ makes the correct yield tend to $1/q$ while the wrong fraction tends to zero. This result assumes successful delivery of each compiled gate. With an individual failure cap of $u$ on each, account for as much as $ru$ total timeout probability. There is no bound uniform in $q$ asserted for the purifying depth. At $q=12$, 64 comparisons in the tested family gave joint bright yield about $0.08528$ with $0.02317$ incorrect fraction. At 1024 the correct joint mass was $0.0833231$ and the wrong fraction about $3.0\times10^{-31}$. [All finite counts, posterior tails, retuned settings and timeout controls](results/rational_phase_comparison.json)

Compare $T^d$ to $I$ on the retained plane to read the signed contrast $\cos(d\alpha)$. Comparing $T^d$ to the exact signed permutation $J$ reads $\sin(d\alpha)$. Although the individual real conjugate components assign opposite signs to the imaginary unit, the two labels change together and their readouts agree. A wrong conditional mass $\eta$ changes either contrast by at most $2\eta$. Conditioning two sheets against the same plane similarly aligns their chiral labels; conditional on that plane the two tests factor for localized product inputs. Joint powers give the angles $2\pi(d_1/q_1+d_2/q_2)$, including arbitrary multiples of the unit angle of the least common multiple via Bézout's identity. Independently calibrated planes with uncorrelated orientations do not guarantee this correspondence.

The field generated **by the limiting contrasts** at fixed order is $\mathbb Q(\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha)=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{\operatorname{lcm}(q,4)})^+$. Adjoining $i$ gives $\mathbb Q(\zeta_q,i)$, while the field before adjoining $i$ is real, so it is exactly the subfield fixed by complex conjugation. For $q=5$ the real degree is four, versus two for the cosine alone. Every finite synchronized coordinate transcript of the constructed apparatus has rational probability, including a completed retry word. The larger field describes limits in the ordered real probabilities. It does not occur as a field of exact finite readouts from localized rational inputs. A simultaneous relabeling of the held sheet and reference can preserve all statistics. The program still needs physical grounds for these controls, their squared readout and the relation between the constructed real limits and independently named amplitudes.
