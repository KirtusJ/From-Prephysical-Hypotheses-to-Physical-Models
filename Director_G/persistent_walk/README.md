[Swapping unequal detector banks](Swapping_unequal_detector_banks.md) · Director G, OpenAI ChatGPT. A fresh optical swap handles unequal detector efficiencies. The [checker](check_swapped_banks.py) includes calibration uncertainty and the power cost of the reference measurement.

[Reading the ten-site circle figure](Reading_the_ten_site_circle_figure.md) · Director G, OpenAI ChatGPT. The [original-figure checker](check_circle_figure.py) compares the numerical and experimental panels of Lorz et al. with sharp and diagonal inputs and identifies which preparation records would resolve the discrepancy.

[Preparation-independent returns on the circle](A_return_independent_of_the_launch_rail.md) · Director G, OpenAI ChatGPT. A reflection argument fixes the antipodal intensity for every localized input rail state. The [two-round checker](check_fixed_site_return.py) also tests a relative detector gain using the sum of two reflected sites in the published figure.

[Three unequal detector gains on the optical circle](Testing_three_unequal_circle_detectors.md) · Director G, OpenAI ChatGPT. Rounds 11 and 13 give incompatible bounds even when the three sites have separate fixed gains and the normalization varies by round. The [checker](check_unequal_circle_gains.py) measures how much widening the color bands weakens the result.

[Different responses to the two outgoing rails](When_the_detector_weights_the_two_rails_differently.md) · Director G, OpenAI ChatGPT. A fixed response fits all sites for a sharp input in rounds 1, 11 and 13. Adding round 7 rules out every such gain assignment for that input. The [checker](check_rail_dependent_circle_response.py) also tests a real diagonal preparation.

[Necessary temporal detector-gain drift](Necessary_temporal_circle_gain_drift.md) · Director G, OpenAI ChatGPT. The [exact-rational checker](check_temporal_gain_drift.py) gives a lower bound on how much the relative site gains must change between rounds if a time-dependent explanation is invoked, and retains the non-excluding round-nine control.
