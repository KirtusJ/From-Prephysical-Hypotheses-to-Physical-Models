## Seven official Clay problem descriptions

All seven linked PDFs opened as full texts. These references specify the questions and their domains. The official Poincaré resolution page separately supplies the later theorem and prize history.

| Problem | What the official description establishes | Full text |
|---|---|---|
| Riemann | The Riemann hypothesis puts every nontrivial zero of ζ on the line Re(s) = 1/2. | [Bombieri, 11 pages](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/riemann.pdf) |
| Hodge | On a nonsingular complex projective variety, every rational Hodge class should be a rational linear combination of algebraic cycle classes. | [Deligne, 5 pages](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/hodge.pdf) |
| Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer | For an elliptic curve over ℚ the predicted order of vanishing at s = 1 equals the rank of its rational points. Wiles distinguishes the refined leading coefficient. | [Wiles, 5 pages](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/birchswin.pdf) |
| Navier–Stokes | Fefferman accepts any one of four alternatives. (A) requires global smooth unforced solutions on ℝ³ for the specified rapidly decaying smooth divergence-free data, with bounded kinetic energy. (B) is periodic existence. Breakdowns (C) and (D) allow forcing. | [Fefferman, 6 pages](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/navierstokes.pdf) |
| P vs NP | Cook asks whether every language accepted by a nondeterministic polynomial-time algorithm is accepted by a deterministic one in polynomial time. G1 adopts the negative answer. | [Cook, 12 pages](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pvsnp.pdf) |
| Yang–Mills | For every compact simple gauge group, the problem asks for a nontrivial quantum Yang–Mills theory on ℝ⁴ with the stated axiomatic properties and a positive mass gap. | [Jaffe and Witten, 14 pages](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf) |
| Poincaré | Every closed simply connected three-manifold is homeomorphic to S³. Milnor gives the historical question.
