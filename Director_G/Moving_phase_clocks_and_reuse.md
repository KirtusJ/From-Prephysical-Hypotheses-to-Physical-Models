**Kirtus Justus**, Theoretical Physicist

The eight-sheet optical comparison needs one arm to acquire an odd delay relative to the other. Two arms starting on the same parity of an even ring and taking one nearest-neighbour hop every tick always finish on the same parity. An odd *physical* displacement between them is impossible. There are two ways to build a comparator once the sheet, routing controls and final readout are specified. They realize different translations. The original optical experiment measured photons. The constructions here are mathematical implementations of its modeled settings. [Earlier phase preparation and experimental link](Preparing_a_phase_clock.md)

## A doubled ring, with a readout at a specified tick

Encode logical cell $j\in\mathbb Z/M$ at physical cell $E_t(j)=2j-t\bmod 2M$. Send branch $b=0$ left and branch $b=1$ right. Then

$$E_t(j)+(2b-1)=E_{t+1}(j+b)\pmod{2M}.$$

At time $t$, the detector converts $y$ to $((y+t)\bmod 2M)/2$ on the occupied parity. This is one-to-one. Applying the identity to each signed history preserves every interfering sum, including destructive cancellations and reads conditional on earlier records. Both arms reach their assigned physical ports simultaneously. An address-dependent splitter, signed recombiner and clean reversible direction control must actually be supplied. A retained record of the route prevents the intended addition of amplitudes. Montero derived a related change of coordinates for the unidirectional walk on the line. [Montero](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.1807) · [Finite signed-history proof](lean/MovingPhaseClock.lean)

A stationary readout at logical boundaries is also possible. Start on $2j$, take the selected left or right hop, then one common right hop. The endpoints are $2j$ and $2(j+1)$. Use a spare direction bit initialized right, flip it on branch zero before the first stream, undo the flip using the preserved branch bit, and stream again. On the opposite initial direction the operation realizes the inverse shift. The complete circuit is a permutation on all $8M$ branch, position and direction states. Local pulses, initialization, and reading at even elapsed times are apparatus assumptions. The moving detector saves a hop per logical step. [Physical motor and readout tests](check_moving_phase_clock.py)

For the eight-cell optical controller the launch split is supplied at the boundary. Six shifts are conditional on the V polarization. Each of the ten original base steps uses three origin-dependent sheet shifts for the nominal 135° plate, then one common sheet hop simultaneous with the base coin and stream. This takes 46 streamed ticks, or 92 with the fixed detector, after launch preparation. For the first four plus-conditioned seeds the origin probabilities are $43/128,73/128,83/128,85/128$. All positions agree with the original logical circuit at each tested setting. This transfers a calibrated relative phase. Every finite squared record in the transfer still has rational mass. The physical generator of the encoded phase is a **two-cell** translation. A logical Fourier mode $k$ maps to equally weighted physical frequencies $k$ and $k+M$ on the doubled ring. The phase between the two Fourier amplitudes is $(-1)^t$. A one-cell shift takes the entire state to an orthogonal parity, so its expectation is zero. At $M=8$ a pure logical mode 1 has physical weights $1/2$ at modes 1 and 9, zero at mode 15. A physical detector selecting only modes 1 and 15 therefore reads $1/2$. Calling the doubled cycle's unit edge the prepared reference would give an incorrect calibration. [Physical Fourier spectra](results/moving_phase_clock.json)

## An odd ring with the original physical unit

On an odd cycle take $u$ to be the least absolute integer with $2u=h\bmod M$. Two unrestricted always-moving arms implementing relative displacement $h$ need at least $|u|$ ticks if a common endpoint translation is allowed. The difference of their signed displacements is even and bounded by twice the elapsed time. Opposite ballistic paths attain the bound. For fixed endpoints $0,h$ the minimum is $2|u|$. At an even horizon the shortest possible even lift for $h$ has magnitude $2|u|$. At an odd horizon a return to 0 costs at least $M$ ticks. For the construction, route oppositely for $|u|$ ticks, then move together for $|u|$ ticks to return the reference arm to its start. Common two-hop excursions pad every setting to $M-1$. Thus a unit delay on the original odd cycle costs $M-1$ hops in a fixed frame. In the exact field scaling $2\pi/M=|F|\varepsilon\delta^3$, $\tau=\varepsilon\delta^2$ per hop, this costs $2\pi/(|F|\delta)-\tau$ before counting local operations. [Exact minimum depths and complementary even cycles](results/odd_phase_clock.json) · [Field units](Forced_envelope_and_classical_motion.md)

Here is an instrument with nonvanishing yield for the original physical Fourier pair $\{\pm1\}$. For $M\ge5$ let $B$ be the largest power of two at most $M/4$. Split coherently into cells $0,2,\ldots,2(B-1)$ with equal amplitudes. Each binary fanout uses the preceding fixed-endpoint transport. The new address bit reversibly erases the branch bit before the next split. Total streamed depth is $2(B-1)$. The initial target weight is

$$W=\frac{2\sin^2(2\pi B/M)}{MB\sin^2(2\pi/M)}\ge\frac{2}{\pi^2}.$$

Indeed $M/8<B\le M/4$. On $[\pi/4,\pi/2]$ the chord bound for $\sin^2 x$ gives $\sin^2 x\ge2x/\pi$. For $M=3$ a single cell already gives $W=2/3$. An arbitrary unknown input can have $W=0$.

For each probe choose $h$ independently and uniformly. Compare $I$ and $T^h$ on the retained clock and read its signed port $Z=\pm1$. Score $X=Z$ when cyclic $|h|\le\lfloor M/4\rfloor$, and $X=-Z$ otherwise. This requires an integer mask and actual port reads. With $L=2\lfloor M/4\rfloor+1$, the conditional mean at folded $k>0$ is $2\sin(\pi kL/M)/(M\sin(\pi k/M))$ by summing the finite cosine series. The mean at $k=0$ is $(2L-M)/M$. At $k=1$ the mean is $1/[M\sin(\pi/(2M))]\ge2/\pi$. At $k=2$ the magnitude is $1/[M\cos(\pi/M)]$ for $M\ge5$. For $3\le k\le M/2$, the bound $M\sin(\pi k/M)\ge2k$ gives magnitude at most $1/3$. Accept a mean score strictly exceeding $1/2$. With $r$ probes the bounded-variable tail inequality gives each class error at most $\eta=\exp(-r/128)$. True joint acceptance is at least $W(1-\eta)$ and wrong joint acceptance at most $(1-W)\eta$. The preparation and probe transport therefore costs $O(M\log(1/\eta))$ hops per launch. A cap on the unbiased random setting source produces a recorded pending outcome. Dark, rejected, and zero-weight branches are included in the finite tests. ## Reusing one prepared clock

Suppose every new flight couples to the same register through an instrument diagonal in its Fourier label $k$. Let $w_k$ denote the starting spectral weights. For every finite transcript $h$, tracing the sheet gives $P(h)=\sum_k w_k P_k(h)$ because different Fourier labels are orthogonal. Fresh base inputs and a common policy for choosing later settings from actual records are included. If the two good conjugate blocks have the same **complete** law $Q$ for those reads, the joint total variation distance to $Q$ is at most their initial wrong weight $e$, for any number of flights. Conditioning on a rare event of probability $s$ can raise the wrong posterior to $e/s$. Equality for individual marginals alone does not imply equality for a collective measurement. [Exact signed adaptive record trees](results/clock_reuse.json)

For example, the real standing wave $(|0\rangle-|2\rangle)/\sqrt2$ on four sheets lies entirely in the target pair. After a path in $|+\rangle$ controls one unit translation, its two clock states are orthogonal. The reduced clock purity is $1/2$. A real Bell measurement on two emitted paths distinguishes sharing this clock from preparing separate copies at total variation distance $1/2$. A pure single character instead acquires only a scalar phase under each translation and factors out. This distinction also arises in independent work on the preservation of a coherence resource and the correlations generated by repeated use. [Complete Bell readouts](results/clock_reuse.json) · [Vaccaro, Croke and Barnett](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.05154)

For $n$ sequential flights the expected preparation cost per flight can scale as $O(r/(n\delta))$, with $r$ probes in the specified force scaling. The first flight still waits for the full preparation. If each good flight has a uniform conditional error at most $\eta_\delta$ against a specified continuum experiment, a joint comparison costs at most $e+n\eta_\delta$. To make both upper bounds vanish one may choose $r/\delta\ll n\ll1/\eta_\delta$ when $r\eta_\delta/\delta\to0$ and $e\to0$. The requisite rate, access by all flights to one physical register, and its propagation between separated sites still need a physical account.
