# A three-walk count test with a correlated middle record

**Kirtus Justus**, Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada. Research supplement by Director G (OpenAI Codex).

A three-bank threshold test can work even when the central record directly copies information from its neighbor. A product source for the two **outer** records, conditional on any shared launch type, suffices over a known range of masses. At a finite-depth local source the two outer records can obey this condition by causal separation. A correlated central flag built from three forward signed walks gives a concrete example with a predicted gap of at least $0.0629$ in original-launch rates. [Three-copy count reference](One_threshold_on_several_walks.md)

## Exactly which outer and middle masses suffice?

Fix a source type. Let $A,M,B$ be binary records in spatial order, with kinematic masses $u,w,v$. These masses are squared norms of commuting record projectors. Suppose $A,B$ factor, and otherwise allow arbitrary correlations of $M$ with either or both. Let $K=A+M+B$ and $s_j=\Pr_{\rm kin}(K\ge j)$. A random threshold $j=1,2,3$ and one pooled, randomly interchanged pair of output ports give the average of $G(s_j)$ for a common nondecreasing curve satisfying $G(1-s)=1-G(s)$. A fixed physical readout and identical original-block denominators across independently assigned settings are required.

**Criterion.** Every admissible table and every such response have threshold rate at most $2/3$ if and only if
$$ \min\{u+v,\ w+uv\}\le1. $$
For a proof, $s_2+s_3=\mathbb E_{\rm kin}(K-1)_+=uv+\Pr_{\rm kin}(M=1,\ A\lor B)$. The latter joint term is at most $\min(w,u+v-uv)$. This bound is attained by assigning the middle mass first to the outer cells with $A\lor B=1$ and any remaining mass to the $00$ cell. Allocations may split a cell. The table can then be realized as a classical diagonal source, so attainability needs no interference assumption. In local hardware, a middle register with time to receive both outer records can implement the assignment with an additional conditional coin. Thus $\sup(s_2+s_3)=\min(u+v,w+uv)$.

If $s_2+s_3\le1$, monotonicity and complementarity give $G(s_2)+G(s_3)\le1$. If $s_2+s_3>1$, necessarily $s_2>1/2$. Put $G(s)=1$ for $s\ge s_2$, $G(s)=0$ for $s\le1-s_2$, and $G(s)=1/2$ in between. Then $G(s_1)=G(s_2)=1$ and $G(s_3)\ge1/2$. These finite evaluations admit a continuous nondecreasing complementary interpolant, with endpoint values zero and one. Thus when the criterion fails there is a source and detector with threshold rate at least $5/6$. The statement classifies the guarantee over possible middle preparations. An individual correlated middle can still pass beyond this range.

## A local apparatus and the distance between outer records

Take an open line of cells, initially product across cells at each specified hidden type. Allow arbitrary within-cell operations and $d\ge1$ layers of gates on disjoint nearest-neighbor pairs. Each endpoint flag must be fully local to its cell by the comparison surface. At cell separation at least $2d$, the outer records factor at that type. A single input cell has a forward influence interval of at most two cells after the first layer, then grows by at most one cell at each boundary per layer. Its diameter is at most $2d-1$. Hence the two pulled-back endpoint projectors act on disjoint input supports and their expectation factors on the conditional product. Arbitrary flags between them may have overlapping supports. A depth-$d$ circuit can correlate flags at separation $2d-1$: create a correlated neighboring pair in the first layer and copy its basis record to the next outer blank on each side in each subsequent layer. A pre-existing shared environment, herald selection or common classical cause can spoil the product premise. The diameter is in **cells**, which can contain complete instruments. Continuous-time Hamiltonian interactions instead admit estimates with tails. [Bravyi, Hastings and Verstraete on correlation propagation](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0603121)

For a realization, use cells $0,1,2$ on an open chain. In the two outer cells run independent four-tick signed Hadamard walks on disjoint lines, or local rings with ten or more wells, each starting on R at well zero. Keep a basis flag for the event at relative well $+2$ or $+4$. At tick four their squared-mass numerators there are $10$ and $1$ out of $16$. In the middle cell run a two-tick walk from R, flag relative well $0$ or $+2$, then hold its state to the common clock. This flag $C$ has mass $3/4$. All three local workspaces and routing records remain present. In one adjacent-cell layer retain the left flag $A$ and reversibly XOR $A\land C$ into a middle blank. The three count inputs are now $A,M=A\land C,B$. This gate can transmit the left flag coherently with a blank wire and a middle-cell Toffoli. It needs no communication between outer cells during source preparation. The threshold instrument later gathers the three protected flags reversibly through local links and reads its pooled flag, so the terminal detector needs time to receive them. The causal argument concerns the prepared flags before that joint measurement. The resource count assumes a bank per cell and within-cell position predicates. Embedding all banks and the control network on one microscopic line would also need their footprint and routing times.

Here $u=v=11/16$, $w=33/64$ and $w+uv=253/256$. The actual threshold masses are
$$ (s_1,s_2,s_3)=(924,649,363)/1024. $$
The sum $s_2+s_3$ attains its upper bound. A continuous response with a sufficiently narrow ramp at one half reaches $2/3$. In a matched separate read choose one of the three banks uniformly, pool that bank's event and complement, and use $G_2(p)=p^2/(p^2+(1-p)^2)$. Its rate is $(2G_2(11/16)+G_2(33/64))/3=327547/448950$, exceeding the three-threshold ceiling by $28247/448950$. The ordinary rule gives mean mass $121/192$ in either scheme. If an experiment measures that single-bank rate and the joint instrument satisfies the threshold assumptions, its single-bank minus threshold rate must be at least the stated gap. A calibrated excess up to $3/256$ in the outer joint $11$ mass over $uv$ cannot overturn the ceiling if the three marginal masses are as given, since $s_2+s_3\le w+\Pr_{\rm kin}(A=B=1)$. These are predictions for a stipulated apparatus.

## What a shared source fluctuation changes

The criterion may be applied at each type and the resulting upper bounds averaged over a common population. Substitution of launch-mean masses into $w+uv$ is unsafe. Keep the same product preparation at the two ends conditional on a hidden low/high setting, with $u=v=1/8$ or $7/8$ respectively and high-type fraction $3/4$. Keep $C$ independent with mass $3/4$ and the same middle rule. These two endpoint masses can also be programmed as complementary predicates of one occupied mass-one site after three walk ticks. The mean marginals are again $11/16,33/64,11/16$. The low-type threshold masses are $(60,25,3)/256$ and the high-type masses are $(252,217,147)/256$. A single continuous complementary detector going from zero at $0.49$ to one at $0.51$ now reports threshold rate $3/4$. Every type separately has disjoint outer causal cones. Population calibration of each individual bank cannot exclude this shared fluctuation.

For many single-cell flags at the same depth, the count-fraction variance at fixed type is at most $(4d-1)/(4m)$ for $m$ distinct output cells. Diagonal variances are at most $1/4$ and both signs of separation $1,\ldots,2d-1$ can contribute off diagonal, each covariance bounded in absolute value by $1/4$. This supplies the concentration condition of the earlier count reference for $d=o(m)$, together with its stipulated endpoint response. A common type can leave a variance of its conditional means after pooling. This example changes the nonlinear observable even though the spatial circuit at fixed type is local. Nothing in the construction identifies a universal detector or an independently named probability embedding.
