Two ordinary signed Hadamard ticks can simulate one logical tick, including a coherent direction filter. The readout uses periodically spaced sets of wells. The underlying signed walk, its cyclic sheet, and its microscopic endpoint score are unchanged. The extra time and rejected trials are measurable costs.

## Why a direct one-tick aperture fails

Take two occupied departure wells $x,x+2$ on an even ring with at least eight wells. A filter on the departure well followed by one on the arrival well accepts an edge exactly when both wells lie in their respective allowed sets. To pass both right edges $x\to x+1$ and $x+2\to x+3$ it must pass $x+2\to x+1$ as well. Thus this pair of sets cannot select R on every extended input. Recording which predecessor supplied the step would change subsequent interference. An uncoupled spectator ring with independent position masks leaves this one-walker edge test unchanged. ## A straight pair on the same microscopic walk

Let the physical sheet have order $N$ and let the base have $4N$ wells. Encode a logical well $x$ as the even physical well $2x$. The logical ring has $2N$ wells. Launch at $2a$ with the given rail. At logical time $j$ the encoded walkers occupy sites congruent to $2(a+j)$ modulo four. Apply two unmodified microticks. At their end retain the physical class $2(a+j)+2\bmod4$, using one yes/no position aperture. An RL or LR pair stays in the old class. RR and LL enter the retained class, advance one logical well in their direction and retain their logical arrival rail.

To select a logical direction at this step, also retain the intermediate class $2(a+j)+1\bmod4$ for R or $2(a+j)-1\bmod4$ for L. The classes are disjoint and cover all intermediate arrivals. This read retains all wells in the selected class coherently under the position-product score premise. For an unmeasured logical direction, leave that intermediate slice unrestricted. A desired logical positional mask can be applied to the doubled sites at a boundary. A sharp port can be recorded by combining its intermediate direction class with its named boundary well. These operations can be scheduled from previous registered outcomes. A device that also retains which of the allowed wells passed implements a different instrument.

For each fixed transcript, admitted histories correspond bijectively to the intended logical words. The map sends a step $e$ to $ee$. All path multiplicities, including cancellations at terminal ports, are retained. In physical Fourier sector $s$ with $z=e^{2\pi is/N}$, the second step of the pair contributes one extra minus sign for L. Its physical departure gains are $x$ and $x-\mathbf1_L$, since the intermediate physical well is $2x\pm1$. If a logical word $w$ of length $t$ has $n_L$ left steps, departure staircase gain $g(w)$ and ordinary Hadamard sign $\epsilon(w)$, then its doubled word has

$$
G(Dw)=4g(w)+2\sum_{j=0}^{t-1}((a+j)\bmod2)-n_L,
\qquad E(Dw)=(-1)^{n_L}\epsilon(w).
$$

Here $Dw$ doubles each individual letter. On the line $n_L=(a+t-x_{\rm end})/2$ is fixed at an endpoint. On the cyclic base two logical lifts of that endpoint differ by a multiple of $2N$, so $n_L$ differs by a multiple of $N$. For even $N$, both $(-1)^{n_L}$ and $z^{-n_L}$ are unchanged. The direct logical staircase $\lfloor x/2\rfloor$ changes by a multiple of $N$ upon adding $2N$ to $x$. This proves the endpoint gauge, including arbitrary winding and negative lifts, for every even $N$. On a nonwrapping flight the argument works without the even-$N$ condition. In particular take $N=4M$. The effective character $z^4=e^{2\pi is/M}$ is the restriction of the original physical character to the logical unit. The address ring has become $8M$ logical wells. This is a larger carrier than the historical $4M$-well model. In applications on a local cone the sizes agree on all visited lifts; otherwise the larger boundary condition must be used. A global order-$N$ label $s$ and $s+M$ give the same encoded response even if other physical preparations distinguish them. Pure count stays count, and the original stationary weights push forward under this explicit restriction.

The double-cover distinction can already be seen with two straight rays from R at zero. At $t=2M$ retain the upper and lower extreme wells at each logical step; from the second step onward this mask admits just the all-R and all-L rays. On the historical $4M$ ring they meet at its antipode from opposite rails. Keep their common R daughter at $t=2M+1$. At the trivial character their signs cancel, giving zero at that port. On the encoded $8M$ ring the two antipodal lifts remain distinct and are read separately; pooling their R daughters sums two nonzero probabilities. Thus ordinary pooling of the two lifts fails to implement their coherent identification.

## Yield and inheritance

Per original launch, any complete passing logical transcript of $t$ steps at a sharp terminal port has probability $2^{-t}$ times its logical probability in the pushed-forward classified law. Both the count numerator and the modulus-squared character numerator equal their logical counterparts, while the microclock denominator is $2^{2t}$ versus logical $2^t$. The same conclusion holds for a terminal sum over oriented ports with ordinary addition of registered outcomes. In particular, without any logical selection the whole recorded sequence of straight-pair passes has yield $2^{-t}$ per original launch at fixed $t$, independent of the sector. Pair failures must be retained separately. Aggregating leaves at different logical stopping times requires their individual factors $2^{-t}$.

For a target rail block the midpoint singleton *class* is one position product, so the classification applies directly without a response postulate for a new pointer register. This is an inheritance result for probabilities on product apertures. It does not establish the physical origin of the parent score or remove the need for a coherent binary aperture that preserves interference inside its allowed set. Within a fixed sector the surviving one-step map is one logical Hadamard tick multiplied by a known scalar and endpoint phase. Continuous simulation using the same encoding cancels such endpoint phases in terminal probabilities. If one instead abandons the encoding and releases directly into a different microscopic sequence, the residual spatial gauge and the altered units must be carried forward. Exponentially small yields at long logical times also prevent importing unit-yield or bounded-mean restart estimates from a direct direction writer. For example, the complete coherent preparation with $m$ two-tick switching blocks uses $4m$ microticks. Its per-attempt yield in a fixed sector is $2^{-2m}P_m$, where $P_m$ is the corresponding logical switching-word probability on the specified ring. Keeping the encoding for $n$ more unrestricted logical flight ticks requires $2n$ more microticks and admits the fraction $2^{-n}$ of that prepared component. Stopping postselection at release gives a different physical flight. A retry after an encoding failure needs a new valid sharp encoded launch and its own timing analysis.
