## The experiment and its transcript

A localized input is one site, one incoming direction and one sheet in \(\mathbb Z/M\), with odd \(M\). A step translates the sheet by the departure site, mixes the directions with the real balanced signed coin, and shifts the walker one site. A native observation projects onto a specified site and direction and leaves the sheets coherent. For a stronger test, allow all coordinate projections, including a reading of the named sheet or a mask of several coordinates. Controls may choose the next operation using earlier outcomes, stop, postselect, restart on a localized input, or use independent copies. The signed contact shift on a shared sheet is also a permutation. Classical randomization over protocols must use the same distribution in the competing models. The label on a button or a sheet stays fixed in the comparison. Coherent control of two paths with different numbers of coin applications is an additional operation.

**Branch invariant.** On a fixed finite branch with \(t\) elementary Hadamard factors the unnormalized state is \(2^{-t/2}m\), with \(m\) a vector of integers in physical coordinates. This follows by induction. A coin adds or subtracts two integer components before division by \(\sqrt2\). A translation permutes them. A coordinate mask retains or zeros each component. In a synchronized product count each elementary coin. A measured restart can be represented by keeping its recorded history and using the product of branch probabilities. No irrational normalizing factor is needed on an intermediate branch.

For any embedding \(\sigma\) of \(K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_M,\sqrt2)\), the integer entries remain fixed and \(2^{-t/2}\) can change only by a global sign. Consequently the cylinder probability is the same rational number in every embedding,
\[
                p_\sigma(b)=2^{-t}\sum_i m_i^2. \tag{1}
\]
A nonzero postselection divides two agreed probabilities. Distinct discarded outcomes add as classical alternatives. The argument also covers any one branch of a controlled experiment whose fixed-coordinate matrices are defined over \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\) when comparing embeddings **fixing** \(\sqrt2\). This broader statement allows coherent mixes of different depths over that field; it does not extend the global-sign argument to such mixes. A sheet label, an asymmetric sheet mask or a sheet-resolved readout can destroy translation symmetry, yet (1) still holds. The proof requires no symmetry of the experiment under a permutation of physical sheet labels.

Every fixed protocol therefore gives identical finite transcript distributions. For countably many rounds with discrete outcomes, the cylinder sets generate the path sigma algebra. The two ordinary probability measures agree there by uniqueness; an absorbing stop symbol includes protocols that sometimes run forever. This extension concerns probabilities of **measured records**. It makes no extension claim for a quantum measure on unmeasured paths. Equal transcript laws imply zero total variation, a likelihood ratio of one on observations of positive probability, and optimal equal-prior discrimination success of \(1/2\). Coupling the models on their common transcript gives the same result for an adaptive protocol or convergence statistic. Ordinary probabilities of observed data also determine the identifiable content of self-consistent gate-set tomography, where the group causing ambiguity is different. [Blume-Kohout et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5330857/)

## What a finite native branch can prepare

At a final vertex write its integer sheet vector as \(m_y\) and \(A(X)=\sum_{y=0}^{M-1}m_yX^y\). Its Fourier amplitudes, apart from a common normalization, are \(A(\zeta_M^r)\). If one vanishes then all characters of the same order vanish. Indeed they are values of one rational polynomial at roots having the same cyclotomic minimal polynomial. Apply this argument separately at every surviving vertex. Thus a positive finite branch cannot occupy just one faithful real plane when \(\varphi(M)>2\). The same conclusion holds for a positive mixture supported only on that plane, since positive summands cannot cancel leakage. This is a restriction on the *support* of a finite preparation and still permits arbitrarily unequal nonzero sector weights. At \(M=3\) there is just one faithful real plane, and the destructive three-step record prepares it exactly. The decomposition of cyclic lifts by their characters and the cyclotomic arithmetic used here are externally established. [Dalfó, Fiol and Širáň](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.04463) · [Conrad on prime-power cyclotomic fields](https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/154Page/handouts/cycint.pdf)

**A sharp five-sheet bound.** At one recorded vertex suppose the constant sector is removed, so \(\sum m_y=0\). Put \(C=\sum m_y^2>0\), \(\zeta=e^{2\pi i/5}\), \(a=|A(\zeta)|^2\), \(b=|A(\zeta^2)|^2\). Orthogonality of the five Fourier characters gives \(a+b=5C/2\). The algebraic norm of \(A(\zeta)\) is \(ab\). It is a positive integer divisible by five: \(A(1)=0\) gives \(A(X)=(X-1)B(X)\) over the integers and \(\prod_{j=1}^4(1-\zeta^j)=5\). Nonvanishing follows from the support result and the removal of the constant sector. If \(e=\min(a,b)/(a+b)\) is the smaller real-plane probability in the ordinary decomposition, then
\[
  e(1-e)=\frac{ab}{(a+b)^2}\ \ge\ \frac4{5C^2},
  \qquad e\ge\frac{1-\sqrt{1-16/(5C^2)}}2 . \tag{2}
\]
Here \(C\) uses the actual integer counts after stripping only the common Hadamard denominator. The branch has joint probability \(C/2^t\) and \(C\le2^t\), so \(e\ge4/(5\cdot4^t)\). Equation (2) is stronger for a known branch and concerns an isolated native output vertex. It does not purport to optimize elapsed time over protocols.

The earlier three-step instrument attains equality in (2) at **every** length of the following word. Take one destructive record and then \(s\) consecutive constructive records, using the appropriate outcome for the current channel. Up to a common sign and a known sheet translation the integer vector is
\[
 A_s(T)|0\rangle=(T+T^{-1})^s(T-T^{-1})|0\rangle .
\]
The norm of \(\zeta-\zeta^{-1}\) is five. The norm of \(\zeta+\zeta^{-1}\) is one because its two real values are \(\phi^{-1}\) and \(-\phi\), each twice among the four conjugates, with \(\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2\). Hence \(a_sb_s=5\). The wrong-plane weight for the target \(r=\pm2\) is \(e_s=a_s/(a_s+b_s)=1/(1+\phi^{4s-2})\), since \(b_0/a_0=4\cos^2(2\pi/5)=\phi^{-2}\). For \(s=0\) the target is the smaller plane. Formula (2) always uses the smaller of the two.

## Testing the selected mode with its own records

Let \(q_\pm=(3\pm\sqrt5)/16\). The next constructive outcome has probability
\[
 q_s=(1-e_s)q_+ + e_s q_-
     =\frac{\|(T+T^{-1})A_s(T)|0\rangle\|^2}
             {8\|A_s(T)|0\rangle\|^2}. \tag{3}
\]
The right side is the directly measurable ratio of two integer counts. For \(s=0,1,2,3,6,10\) it gives respectively \(1/8,1/4,5/16,13/40,233/712,5473/16724\). It tends to \(q_+\). Under \(\zeta\mapsto\zeta^2\), the two real-plane interpretations and their predicted record probabilities interchange. The actual integer sheet vector, its spatial pattern, its next-record frequency and its approach to \(q_+\) are identical. The limit designates an operational preparation by a repeatable record property. An independent assignment of the externally named root \(\zeta\) to that preparation is still required to identify an embedding.

**An ordered number that the walk does select.** This part can be derived in sheet coordinates without choosing a character. Put \(B=T+T^{-1}\). Since \(B^2+B-I=I+T+T^2+T^3+T^4\), this operator vanishes on zero-sum sheet vectors. Multiplication by \(B^2-B-I\) gives \(B^4-3B^2+I=0\) there. With \(d=(T-T^{-1})|0\rangle\), the squared counts \(C_s=d^\mathsf TB^{2s}d\) therefore obey \(C_{s+2}=3C_{s+1}-C_s\), starting at \(C_0=C_1=2\). Thus \(x_s=8q_s=C_{s+1}/C_s\) starts at 1 and iterates \(x\mapsto3-1/x\). The two fixed points are \((3\pm\sqrt5)/2\). Between them the map is increasing and strictly raises \(x\), so \(x_s\) converges to the larger fixed point. Already \(q_1=1/4\) exceeds the smaller root of \(64q^2-24q+1=0\).

There is a second check tied to named physical sheets. Up to its known translation and sign, the filtered vector has coordinates \((0,u_s,v_s,-v_s,-u_s)\). Acting by \(B\) sends \((u_s,v_s)\) to \((v_s,u_s-v_s)\), starting at \((1,0)\). Its eigenvalues are \(\phi^{-1},-\phi\), and the initial vector has a nonzero component on the latter eigenspace. Hence the probability ratio of reading offset 2 to reading offset 1 tends to \(\phi^2\). Here “offset” refers to the measured sheet index after subtracting the known common translation of the branch. This readout independently distinguishes two spatial profiles. The same physical offsets and the same ratio occur in both embedding theories.

The native construction thereby specifies a particular real number by a sequence of rational record ratios. If the real generator \(t\) of an abstract field with \(t^2+t-1=0\) is **defined** by \(t=8\lim q_s-2\), it has the positive value \((\sqrt5-1)/2\). Both theories agree with that definition. What differs is the evaluation of a separately specified expression \(\zeta+\zeta^{-1}\). Requiring that expression to denote this record-defined generator is an additional identification. This distinction matters when using completion: the record sequence orders its own limit even though it supplies no statistical evidence favoring a global embedding.

For each finite \(s\) the prescribed word has positive probability \(C_s/8^{s+1}\). Demanding that the same infinite word occur has probability zero, since a constructive record has probability at most \(1/2\) on each block. Repetition on fresh copies for fixed \(s\) is meaningful. Alternatively an ordinary, unforced stream of records can converge almost surely to a random sector class in the supplied nondemolition model. This can reveal a class-specific limiting frequency, including an irrational one. Its distribution and its relation to other native records still obey the transcript equality. [Bauer and Bernard](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.4953)

Two adverse controls locate the boundary. First, an externally fixed tag \(r=2\) has constructive probability \(q_+\) or \(q_-\) on its first three steps in the two theories. That tag deliberately violates the native preparation condition. Second, single-step direction readings give conditionally fair bits even with sheet postselection in the past, provided the incoming direction is known and no subsequent postselection biases the sequence. Using fresh sequential direction readings and a classical controller, compare the binary expansion of a uniform number with a preprogrammed threshold \(q_+\). The comparison stops at the first differing bit, with probability \(2^{-n}\) of still running after \(n\) bits and expected length two. It produces exactly \(q_+\) in **both** theories. An irrational event probability at unbounded stopping time is therefore compatible with equal laws and rational finite cylinders. The controller has specified the cut in advance. Exact bit sampling is well established independently of this application. [Devroye and Gravel](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.02539)

## A criterion for an enlarged apparatus

For any specified finite algebraic apparatus with \(K\)-valued input rays and complete \(K\)-valued measurement matrices, form its ordinary *normalized* cylinder probabilities \(p_b\) in \(F=K\cap\mathbb R\). For conjugated amplitude laws on the same specified algebraic descriptions the other predictions are \(\sigma(p_b)\), since \(\sigma\) commutes with conjugation and division by a nonzero initial norm. Define \(E=\mathbb Q(p_b:\ b\text{ a finite cylinder of an allowed protocol})\). Two embeddings have identical record statistics precisely when their restrictions to \(E\) agree. If they differ on \(E\), at least one finite cylinder probability differs; equality on the generators implies equality on the generated field. This criterion pertains to primitive experiments or parameter-independent control choices, with no external probability parameter inserted as an embedding-dependent input.

For the native localized protocol (1) gives \(E=\mathbb Q\). An apparatus that prepares the tagged \(r=2\) five-sheet sector, even as an unnormalized ray, puts \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\) in \(E\) via its one record. That distinguishes the two root interpretations but gives no test of the sign of the separate \(\sqrt2\) generator in \(K\). For the finite abelian extensions here full identification on \(F\) is equivalent to \(E=F\) by Galois correspondence. On a general menu one must compute normalized cylinder probabilities: an asymmetric amplitude can cancel with its reference in every normalized observable. Physical grounds for promoting a tag, an ordered angular reference, or a new preparation to a primitive operation remain open.
