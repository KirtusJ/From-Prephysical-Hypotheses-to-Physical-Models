# How much unknown error in a detector pair law can a test withstand?

**Kirtus Justus**, Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada. Research supplement prepared by OpenAI Codex for Director G.

At a cap of four quadrillion pairs, the largest uniformly tolerable total variation radius for 5% conditional error is bracketed by **8.29577 × 10⁻¹¹** (achievable) and **8.29617 × 10⁻¹¹** (impossible). The gap is about 0.00482% of the lower endpoint. Here total variation is half the sum of absolute differences between the four outcome probabilities. The radius applies separately to each model at every allowed mass, conditional on the past. Pair marginals may also shift. The tester knows the nominal tables and the radius. The direction of each perturbation is unknown.

### A test against every allowed direction

Fix mass approximately 0.123279641138. Let P and Q be its nominal four-cell distributions. Group cells attaining the smallest and largest nominal likelihood ratios P/Q. Construct L by moving mass δ from the highest group of P to the lowest, and R by moving δ from the lowest group of Q to the highest. Distribute each transfer proportionally to nominal Q within each tied group. The checker verifies positivity and verifies by exact rational comparisons that these groups still attain the minimum and maximum of L/R. Use log(L/R) as the pair score. Collect the full cap, choose P for a positive sum, Q for a negative sum, and split ties fairly.

At any nontrivial score cutoff x between score levels, the event {score ≤ x} includes the lowest group and excludes the highest. Its probability under L is exactly its probability under P plus δ. No law within δ of P can give the event greater probability. Under R its probability is exactly that under Q minus δ, which no law within δ of Q can undercut. The extremes outside the score range have identical probabilities for all four laws. Thus L is worst for choosing P and R is worst for choosing Q at every score cutoff simultaneously.

This order holds conditional on every preceding history. Using a fresh uniform variate per step for a quantile coupling, the actual score under P dominates an iid L-score at each step; under Q it is dominated by an iid R-score. When a score corresponds to several cells, a further draw within that score group reconstructs the actual cell history. The sum and the sign decision preserve the order, including with a shared fair tie coin. Dependence in the deviations is therefore covered as long as the conditional radius holds. At the achievable endpoint the [checker](check_unknown_tv_direction.py) bounds the worse conditional error under the two iid witnesses below 0.049999999612 via the [finite-sample likelihood calculation](check_fixed_exposure.py). Least-favorable distributions are a standard robust-testing construction. [Background on robust testing](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40300-020-00185-3)

### Excluding larger radii across the menu

At the upper endpoint the checker constructs one admissible pair of alternative tables at every mass. It tries the same transfer first. Where ordering or positivity fails, it shifts each table toward the other by at most δ, stopping at their midpoint if attainable. It verifies all four-cell laws and displacements exactly. Identical controls carry zero information. These alternatives depend only on the selected mass and require no history-dependent adversary.

For every informative table pair, the checker recomputes both directional divergences and checks the maximum log increment. Substitution into the [adaptive clock comparison](Adaptive_exposure_for_two_paired_detector_laws.md) gives equal-prior error above 0.050000001876 for every capped policy, even if the shifted tables are disclosed. At least one conditional error then exceeds 5%. Nested TV neighborhoods extend this impossibility to every larger radius. The lower-endpoint guarantee likewise extends to all smaller radii using the same test.

The proof concerns the finite routed menu and the two nominal detector populations at M = 10⁻⁸. The achievable test uses the entire pair budget; its certificate is separate from the earlier early-stopping schedule's mean savings. The critical radius under a requirement that both detector marginals stay fixed remains a separate question. No physical detector data were acquired.
