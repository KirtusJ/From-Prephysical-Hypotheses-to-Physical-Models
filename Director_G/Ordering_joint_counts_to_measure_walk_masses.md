# Measuring walk masses by ordering joint detector counts

**Kirtus Justus**, Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada. Research supplement by Director G (OpenAI Codex).

A detector of unknown response can measure the squared mass of a walk outcome by comparing it against several known reference amplitudes. The science label and reference label are read together once, on each launch. If the detector scores all joint cells by the same strictly increasing function on that launch, the order of their frequencies gives the order of their masses. The function can fluctuate across launches. Its slope, an exponent and agreement with a separate calibration setting are unnecessary for the population comparison. On a finite signed walk one can recover the entire mass vector exactly from ties in this joint read. Its own measured marginal can then be tested against the recovered vector. ## Exactly what a joint panel determines

At one fixed science setting let \(h_i\ge0\), \(\sum_i h_i=1\), be the kinematic squared masses of its complete reported bins, including any specified loss sector. These can be squared norms of vectors entangled with a spectator. Encode the science bin in an orthogonal register without changing these masses. Adjoin a known independent reference state with squared masses \(r_j\). Keep the science labels and their unobserved states isolated from the reference operations. A read resolves \((i,j)\), giving **kinematic** table \(x_{ij}=h_i r_j\). The reference amplitudes and this product factorization are assumptions about the apparatus. They do not specify its display frequencies.

For each launch type let \(f(0)=0\) and \(f\) be strictly increasing on the tested scalar masses. The probability for cell \((i,j)\) is \(f(x_{ij})/\sum_{a,b}f(x_{ab})\). Different launches can use different \(f\). They expose the same kinematic table, resolve the same cells and preserve their labels. One may even use nondecreasing functions provided each distinct pair of tested masses is separated strictly on a set of types of positive measure, with positive total weight on every launch. The sums use the whole exposure. Hidden subdivision of a cell before applying \(f\), or changing the science state with the detector type, can invalidate the result.

**Finite criterion.** A proposed \(h\) is compatible with the observed normalized joint table \(P\) under the stated scalar class exactly when:

1. \(P_{ij}=0\) precisely at \(h_i r_j=0\).
2. For every pair of cells, equality and strict ordering between their \(P\) values agree with equality and strict ordering between the proposed products.

Indeed the difference of any two normalized responses has the same sign on every type (strictly on a positive fraction for a strict comparison). Averaging gives the forward implication. Conversely, send each distinct tested scalar product to the observed probability of any cell having it. The conditions make this well-defined and strictly increasing after adjoining \((0,0)\). Linear interpolation and positive-slope continuation beyond the largest tested mass give one admissible response. Its weights already sum to one. This also shows that, for finite menus, knowing exact frequencies gives no further restrictions on \(h\) in this class. For fixed \(r\), the candidate set in the normalized simplex is specified by linear equalities and strict linear inequalities in \(h\). The criterion can also reject a putative response model when no candidate satisfies it.

## An adjustable reference built with signed paths

Choose \(K=2^s\). Use an address register with \(K\) states labelled \(k=1,\ldots,K\) and \(2K\) data slots \(y=0,\ldots,2K-1\). Start both registers blank. Apply \(s\) Hadamards to the address and \(s+1\) to the data to obtain uniform amplitudes. On the data slot write the phase \(+1\) for \(y<K+k\) and \(-1\) for \(y\ge K+k\), controlled by the retained address. Apply Hadamards again to the \(s+1\) data bits. At output \((k,0)\) the signed sum is \(2k\), so its squared mass is
\[
 r_{k,0}=\frac{1}{K}\left(\frac{2k}{2K}\right)^2=\frac{k^2}{K^3}.
\]
All other cells are retained. This uses \(2K^2\) reference slots, \(3s+2\) binary Hadamards and a known sign pattern. A reversible comparator can compute the sign predicate, phase a marked slot and clear its scratch before recombination. These are controlled signed gates. A bare nearest-neighbor walk with a fixed coin need not implement them. They act independently of the unknown science amplitudes. The research program has explicit routing constructions for signed matchings; their time and hardware resources matter in a proposed implementation. [Signed comparison circuits](Protected_records_and_coordinate_weights.md) · [Amy et al., exact synthesis of real Toffoli–Hadamard circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11305)

Let \(a\) be a science cell with maximal \(P_{a,(K,0)}\). Then it also has maximal \(h_a>0\). Comparing the counts at \((i,(k,0))\) and \((a,(l,0))\) compares \(h_i/h_a\) with \(l^2/k^2\). If \(h_i=n_i^2/\sum_j n_j^2\) for signed integers with \(|n_i|\le K\), each positive ratio is exposed by an equality at \(k=|n_a|\), \(l=|n_i|\). Find one such pair for each occupied \(i\) and normalize the recovered ratios to sum to one. The signs and the values of the integers were unnecessary in the read. Without such a bound or when a bin contains a general sum of squares, the same apparatus gives intervals. Setting \(k=K\) alone gives a grid in \([0,1]\) of maximal spacing \((2K-1)/K^2\). Intersect the resulting inequalities with all available comparisons, including those between nonanchor cells. Arbitrary precision on fixed positive ratios is possible as \(K\) grows even if the common response changes between experiments, provided the actual science preparation and reference factorization are maintained.

For finite counts one can use simultaneous confidence intervals for the joint probabilities, obtained under a stated sampling model. If the lower endpoint of one exceeds another's upper endpoint, the corresponding strict mass comparison holds on the simultaneous coverage event. Impose only certified comparisons. An unresolved tie supplies no such equality. A conservative alternative is to retain each compatible ordered partition of the cells allowed by the intervals and apply the finite criterion. With no lower bound on response slope, a nearly flat response can require arbitrarily many launches to resolve even a large mass ratio. For instance \(f_\epsilon(x)=1+\epsilon x\) at \(x>0\), with \(f_\epsilon(0)=0\), makes all positive-cell frequency differences tend to zero as \(\epsilon\downarrow0\). Exact ties in this paper concern population frequencies.

## A smaller reference can leave the wrong science mass undetected

Use two science bins with actual masses \((5/8,3/8)\) and an independent unwrapped five-tick sharp-right Hadamard flight as the reference. The distinct occupied reference masses are \(1,4,16\) over 32. Consider the complete ordinary joint histogram. Replacing the science vector by \((3/4,1/4)\) preserves the full product ordering, including ties and dark ports. A single strictly increasing response sends the alternative products in increasing order to their values in the observed table. For example it maps \(1/128\) to \(3/256\), \(3/128\) to \(5/256\), and \(1/32\) to \(3/64\). The other knots are in the replay. This example permits different nuisance responses in the two explanations of the same data. At \(K=4\) the controlled reference has a ratio \(9/4\) between its marked cells at \(l=3\) and \(k=2\). The true ratio \(5/3\) lies below it while the alternative ratio 3 lies above. These two science preparations can therefore be separated by the ordering of just two joint counts. Neither of their ordinary frequencies needs to be presumed.

For a complete science example use an eleven-well reflecting line, source \(5,R\), and five forward ticks. Its absolute directed-mode numerators are at most four. With the \(K=4\) reference the ordering recovers all 22 masses exactly, including dark ports. In a half-mixture of the identity response and \(f(x)=x+x^2\), the recovered masses still agree exactly with the kinematic calculation. Summing the *displayed* joint counts over the reference gives a science marginal at total variation distance \(527/90564\) from that calculation. Agreement in order therefore lets this panel expose a frequency error. It does not by itself force the responding functions to be linear. Even when this augmented panel returns the ordinary marginal, a detector that changes its response when the reference is removed can give different unaugmented frequencies.

The bounds on \(h_i\) can be used directly in the [three-forward-tick instrument certificate](Certifying_a_position_read_with_three_forward_ticks.md). Joint comparisons bound each required kinematic sharp-mode or dark-port mass, and support-only tests continue to distinguish exact zeros. Grouping physical failure modes into one science bin requires a calibrated read with the stipulated common scalar response after pooling there. The detector-error interval and the reference preparation cost can grow with the number of cells. Inferring physical trajectories still calls for matched preparations and reproducible intermediate instruments. A relabeling of cyclotomic embeddings that preserves all joint products remains indistinguishable in these tests.

The [checker](check_joint_mass_order.py) compares microscopic paths and site updates, replays the reference by Walsh butterflies and direct signed sums, constructs the strict interpolant for the smaller reference and retains the full joint table for the nonlinear detector. A [Lean 4.19 certificate](lean/JointMassOrder.lean) checks the four marker sums, the complete order on the two three-level-reference tables and the sign reversal at the new threshold. The [result](results/joint_mass_order.json) records exact rational model outputs. No joint reference counts from a physical walk have been acquired.
