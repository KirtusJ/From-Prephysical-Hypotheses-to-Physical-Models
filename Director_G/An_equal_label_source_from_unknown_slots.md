# Extracting equal routing labels from a source of unknown bias

**Kirtus Justus**, Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada. Research supplement by Director G (OpenAI Codex).

[Two local switches](Two_local_switches_and_one_spectator.md) constrain an unknown detector score when the source supplies equal routing masses. Here five input slots can replace three calibrated balanced splits. Four reversible source tests can certify the symmetry required for the construction. The preparation is conditional, and both the success rate and the response of the unsuccessful population matter. No physical implementation or measured success rate is claimed.

## What the preparation uses

Take $n$ two-state slots prepared in $(\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle)^{\otimes n}$. The complex coefficients can be unknown, and can change between launches. This assumes repeated preparation or an independently supplied symmetric source; a single unknown ray does not supply copies of itself by unitary cloning. The walker and blank routing memory start in known coordinates. In a word with $k$ ones the amplitude is $\alpha^{n-k}\beta^k$. Any fully symmetric pure source has a common word amplitude $b_k$ in each layer, and the following permutation works for it too.

For $M=2^m$, order the $\binom nk$ words of each layer in a fixed order and form as many full groups of $M$ as fit. Map word $j$ of each full group reversibly to a pass flag, a junk coordinate identifying its layer and group, and logical label $j=0,\ldots,M-1$. Map leftover words to distinct fail coordinates. All assignments are injective and can be completed to a permutation on a padded finite bank. A bank of controlled bit permutations, blank memory and a faithful pass/fail instrument are apparatus assumptions beyond a fixed walk coin. They can be tested independently. Project on the pass coordinate while preserving its internal amplitudes; alternatively, for purely label-controlled routing, preserve the conditional label diagonals stated below. Among successful preparations the amplitude is proportional to
$$\left(\frac1{\sqrt M}\sum_{j=0}^{M-1}|j\rangle\right)\otimes\sum_{k,g}\sqrt M\,b_k|k,g\rangle.$$
The factorization also holds for purifications with symmetric slots and environment vectors $b_k$. The pass norm may vanish. A density matrix commuting with all permutations need not be supported on their symmetric subspace. An exchangeable diagonal ensemble gives equal label diagonals after grouping and can reproduce the routed site tables with an incoherent logical output. A subsequent interference comparison needs a coherent preparation. These are different physical uses of the same word permutation. For inference about a mixture of nonlinear scores, the required equality of accepted label diagonals must hold within each responding type, or the detector must score the specified whole density matrix. A uniform mixture of hidden sharp labels looks exchangeable in aggregate. A detector acting on each sharp component can reproduce all ordinary routed site rates at any positive exponent. A herald whose efficiency depends on the label can similarly spoil the conditional equality.

For independent identical slots with $p=|\beta|^2$ the squared norm in pass is
$$Y(p)=\sum_{k=0}^n M\left\lfloor\binom nk/M\right\rfloor p^k(1-p)^{n-k}.$$
This is the kinematic norm, not an independently derived frequency of heralds. The count is optimal at each $p$ among fixed, deterministic permutations and word-diagonal pass selections yielding equal logical masses for **every** unknown bias. Indeed, the difference between labels $i,j$ is $\sum_k(c_{ik}-c_{jk})p^k(1-p)^{n-k}$. Division by $(1-p)^n$ gives a polynomial in $p/(1-p)$. Equality for all interior biases forces $c_{ik}=c_{jk}$ in each layer. At most $\lfloor\binom nk/M\rfloor$ words per label can fit there. The construction attains every bound and aligns the phases in the same junk slot. Extra random settings whose labels are averaged only after a trial, input-dependent programs, or a source of pre-existing balanced amplitudes change the optimization problem. Classical unbiased-bit extraction and coherent type-class projection have related counting principles. [von Neumann](https://mcnp.lanl.gov/pdf_files/InBook_Computing_1961_Neumann_JohnVonNeumannCollectedWorks_VariousTechniquesUsedinConnectionwithRandomDigits.pdf) · [Seroussi and Weinberger](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.2192) · [Bennett et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.53.2046)

The first dyadic bank with $2\le k<M/2$ in the two-switch experiment has $M=8$. Through four slots the largest layer has size six. Five slots supply ten weight-two and ten weight-three words. Retain eight from each, sixteen words total, with a single group at either weight. The resulting norm is $8p^2(1-p)^2$, equal to $1/2$ at $p=1/2$ and $9/32$ at $p=1/4$. It approaches zero as either input state becomes rare. More slots permit larger grids: for every fixed $M$, some layer eventually has at least $M$ words. A physical readiness or resource bound still needs an observed yield.

## Testing source symmetry without a supplied numerical probability law

Suppose every launch is prepared from the same complete blank ray by a reversible unitary $U$, possibly type-dependent, and the very same realization of $U^\dagger$ is available on an audit launch. Randomly choose the production branch or one audit. Audit $i$ swaps neighboring slots $i,i+1$ using the actual slot permutation $S_i$, reverses the preparation and reads the complete blank coordinate versus every other outcome. A no-swap round trip calibrates the baseline. The read must be faithful to zeros: positive weight on the complement gives a positive probability of reporting an outcome there for each type. If some returned sources, environments or loss channels are unobserved this implication requires separate support.

Let $\psi=U|0\rangle$. If every audit has exactly zero probability of leaving blank, positivity and a common population across settings give $S_i\psi=s_i\psi$, $s_i=\pm1$, for every adjacent swap almost surely. (Each swap is a self-adjoint involution.) The braid relation $S_i S_{i+1}S_i=S_{i+1}S_iS_{i+1}$ makes the signs equal. For $n\ge3$ a nonzero fully antisymmetric vector of binary slots is impossible: every binary word contains two equal symbols, so transposing their positions fixes the word and would negate its coefficient. This also holds for vector-valued coefficients if the swaps act trivially on the other systems. Therefore the source is symmetric within each qualifying launch and production can use the factorization above. The test certifies symmetry, not independence, identical product form, or nonzero yield. With two slots the singlet passes both the identity and the single swap return test. Hidden modes that enlarge each swapped slot to dimension at least $n$ can carry the sign representation even for larger $n$. If the inverse tracks a different preparation or the putative blank is a multi-dimensional readout subspace, a blank report need not identify the source ray. The literature also constructs tests for symmetry under given group representations. [LaBorde, Rethinasamy and Wilde](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.12758)

**Finite errors.** Faithfulness to dark outputs alone gives no numerical conversion. For five qubits set $\psi=(|00000\rangle+|10000\rangle)/\sqrt2$. Its squared norm outside the symmetric subspace is $2/5$. The first swap has squared return overlap $1/4$ and the other swaps fix it. Complete the remaining columns of $U$ so the residual from the first swap is balanced across the 31 nonblank echo coordinates. This is possible by a change of orthonormal basis on $\psi^\perp$. With one common strictly increasing score $f(w)=w^q$ on the **full** 32-coordinate read before pooling, the nonblank probability is $31(3/31)^q/[1+31(3/31)^q]$. It tends to zero as $q$ grows. Pooling the actual masses into two cells before the power is a different detector.

A calibrated upper bound $\epsilon$ on each of the four **per-type kinematic** leakages $1-|\langle\psi,S_i\psi\rangle|^2$ gives a finite guarantee. Choose the sign $s_i$ of each real expectation. Then $\|(S_i-s_i)\psi\|\le d=\sqrt{2\epsilon}$. Telescoping the two length-three braid words gives $|s_i-s_{i+1}|\le6d$. For $\epsilon<1/50$ all signs agree. Average the telescoping error for an adjacent-swap word over the 120 permutations, using a shortest word for each. Its average length is five, since each of ten pairs is inverted in half the permutations. Hence $\|\psi-P_\pm\psi\|\le5d$ for the corresponding symmetrizer or antisymmetrizer. The negative projector is zero and $5d<1$, excluding that sign. The nonsymmetric squared mass is at most $50\epsilon$. A measured mean error over types does not of itself give these four per-type upper bounds. For accepted norm $Y>0$, the fraction of kinematic mass outside the target logical ray after passage is at most $\min(1,50\epsilon/Y)$: the pass map is a contraction and its symmetric input component lands exactly in that ray.

## What an original launch can establish

Commit the pass flag first. On a representative set of accepted launches choose the four two-switch settings independently of the hidden detector and source, and read the spectator. In an ideal normalized accepted preparation each label has mass $1/M$. For $M=8$, two packets of three labels each give spectator rates $2/7,2/9,2/9,2/11$ under a squared site-mass score, with contrast $16/693$. These rates use the original **accepted** launches assigned to the settings, including later misses if calibrated. Keep failed preparation attempts counted as well. If the true pass probability at source/detector type $t$ is $h_t$, the contrast in joint pass-and-spectator probabilities per incident start is $E[h_t C_t]$ under downstream assignment independent of the herald. The settings may be chosen after the herald, with assignment probabilities explicitly accounted for. $C_t\ge0$ for the whole-site scalar-score family of the two-switch paper, so exact zero constrains each type with $h_t>0$ almost surely. The conditional contrast is divided by $E[h_t]$ where this is positive. It implies no per-type bound on undetected, never-heralded sources. If the herald itself takes away logical support or later readout scores an unnormalized pass branch jointly with rejection bins, this is a different instrument.
