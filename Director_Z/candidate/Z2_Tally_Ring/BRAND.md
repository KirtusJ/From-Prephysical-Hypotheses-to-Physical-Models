# Tally Ring (candidate Z2)

**One line.** A ring of places, two directions of travel, and a tally carried by every history; what is seen
is the squared size of the tallies that arrive together.

**The name.** Every path through the world multiplies up a *tally*: one element of a small group for each step,
depending only on which way the step turned. The world is a *ring*: a finite circle of places. Nothing else is
in it.

**Mark.** Z2. In formulas: the Tally Ring with ring size N, tally group C, turn tallies (a, b, c, d) and voice
chi, written TR(N; C; a, b, c, d; chi).

## What it is

- A complete toy universe: its space (a ring of N places), its time (ticks), its content (histories and their
  tallies), its one law of appearance (the squared size of summed tallies), and its instruments (screens that
  let through only chosen places at chosen ticks, and a final reading of place and direction).
- Completely stipulated. Seven stipulations, numbered, each of which can be attacked alone.
- Exactly computable. Every statistic in it is a rational combination of roots of unity.
- A world whose inhabitants are limited in a way that can be proved: the theorem at its centre says what they
  can never find out about their own world.

## What it is not

- Not our universe, and not a model of any experiment. Where a number in it matches a laboratory number, that
  is a comparison and nothing more.
- Not a derivation of the squared rule. The squared rule is stipulation T6. The candidate does not say why.
- Not an explanation of quantum mechanics or of classical mechanics, emergent or otherwise.
- Not an ontology. The research program's goal is not reached by it, and no gate is passed.
- Not a repair of candidate Z1. Z1 is abandoned and stays abandoned; Z2 uses only mathematics that survived.

## The one thing it offers to be judged by

Inside the Tally Ring, with instruments that select places, exactly one real number about the world's voice can
be read: the real part of one power of one of its values. Not which of two mirror-image voices the world has,
not the group the tallies live in, not two of the four turn tallies. And the world cannot build, out of its own
steps and screens, an instrument that reads more. If that is false, the candidate is dead.
