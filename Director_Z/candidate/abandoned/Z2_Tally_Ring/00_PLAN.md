## Why this candidate, and what "completely stipulated" means

Candidate Z1 was abandoned because it said the squared rule "follows" while two of its stipulations already
contained it. Z2 takes the opposite course: **everything that is not proved is written down as a stipulation,
the squared rule and the norm-preserving step included.** The candidate does not try to explain why its
stipulations hold. Its worth, if it has any, is in what follows from them, in what its own inhabitants could
and could not find out, and in whether it can be broken.

It is a toy universe: a small world with its own space, time, matter-like content and instruments. Inside it
are toy models: particular choices of ring, group and gains that can be computed exactly.
