\textbf{Scope}\par
Notes on interval restrictions of infinite-chain Wilson–Dirac occupied-band projectors: cross-boundary trace-class estimates, exact spectral majorants, reference geometry and the two-bond asymptotic, Fourier-space boundary defects, the Hilbert–Schmidt scaling limit, square-root-mass control, majorant moments and boundary corrections, and positive reference-modular traces, followed by a note that bounds the continuum cross-block interpolants on a fractional Sobolev space. Each statement keeps its stated infinite-chain, filling and scaling conditions.\par\vspace{5mm}
