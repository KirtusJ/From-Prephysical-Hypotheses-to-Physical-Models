\textbf{Abstract}\par
For finite conventional models \textemdash{} probability kernels, hidden-state models, quantum states and continuous-time Markov generators \textemdash{} the paper determines which features are fixed by a stated menu of observations and interventions. Results include an exact criterion for controlled reduction and a complete-history bound under uniform row error; a parity-shift process with exact innovation coordinates; classical realization rank with finite-sample certificates; anytime-valid likelihood-mixture tests for complete record laws; nonidentification of fragment factorization from a final channel; sharp control criteria for positive hidden realizations; calibration-induced identification intervals; and, for sampled generators, two positive generators with all integer-time samples equal, exact identification from two incommensurate times, and the absence of uniform inverse stability over unbounded rates. Identifiability within a menu does not establish that the chosen states, controls or time coordinate describe a particular physical system.\par
