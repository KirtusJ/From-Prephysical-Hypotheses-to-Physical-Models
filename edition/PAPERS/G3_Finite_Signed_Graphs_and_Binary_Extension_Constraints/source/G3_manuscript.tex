\textbf{Abstract}\par
Finite signed-graph models are studied as mathematical comparators. For a graph double-well functional built from the signless Laplacian, an exact threshold separates the unique trivial minimum from nonzero minimizers, and zero-action configurations of a connected graph exist exactly when the graph is bipartite. Action-count statistics are computed for trees and cycles through an even-subgraph formula, together with a reduced sign law and its conditional mixedness. Complete-bipartite spectra illustrate dependence on the chosen operator. One four-sign law has unique five- and six-coordinate exact extensions and no seven-coordinate exact extension; sharp finite and infinite approximation distances form an odd\textendash even staircase, and an exact action-range tradeoff is given. Quadratic weights are not closed under deletion. A variational minimum does not by itself choose a sampling law or an observation instrument.\par
