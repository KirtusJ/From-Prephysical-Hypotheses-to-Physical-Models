\textbf{Scope}\par
The synthesis states, with examples, which properties of a specified model are fixed by a specified family of observations. The supporting notes that follow it give the detailed arguments for individual examples: source, gain and detector ambiguity, graph and scale observations, predictive dimension and history-dependent readout, and constraints on phase contrast, purity and paired-input data for conditional operations. Each note is restricted to its stated state space, observation menu and calibration premises.\par\vspace{5mm}
