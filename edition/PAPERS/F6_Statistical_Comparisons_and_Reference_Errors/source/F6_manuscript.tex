\textbf{Abstract}\par
Source sampling, query sampling, report errors and errors in a comparison reference are separated in specified mathematical experiments. Exact overlap counting gives the variance of complete collision statistics, and two sources with matching pair statistics are compared through triple collisions. A half-credit score extends triple equality to inconsistent pair reports and has a sharp single-report sensitivity. Sparse random queries are analysed with the complete variance, including the requirement that the report population be fixed before the queries. Reference sampling with a quality gap gives finite-population calibration guarantees, and a minimum worst-case query cap is proved for separating adjacent error counts. All numerical guarantees concern the specified experiments; they are not results of physical trials.\par
