\textbf{Scope}\par
Five articles on the discrete concentration problem for the half-filled sine kernel: higher-order modular periods, concentration eigenvalues from logarithmic connection coefficients, spectral tails and exact concentration logits, reciprocal traces and the plunge law, and positive moment bounds on growing plunge windows. They supply reference-kernel inputs used in the Wilson–Dirac relative-entropy papers.\par\vspace{5mm}
