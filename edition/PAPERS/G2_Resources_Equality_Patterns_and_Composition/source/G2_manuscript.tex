\textbf{Abstract}\par
Classical probability models are used to separate invariance, consistency, observable information and controllable communication. One common normalized reference and a bound on the relative entropy of the complete preparation bound the accessible information; marginal budgets alone do not. Entropy under a mean-cost constraint is finite under a Gibbs summability condition, and an explicit example has a finite entropy supremum without a maximizer. Paintbox families can agree through any prescribed finite order before separating. The entropy of quotient outputs is distinguished from controllable messages. Deterministic operations of fixed finite arity on finite equivalence relations that are natural under every restriction are classified, and a stochastic selection theorem with a local bound is proved. Counts of positions or repetitions are not assigned physical time, spatial volume or energy without a separate resource model.\par
