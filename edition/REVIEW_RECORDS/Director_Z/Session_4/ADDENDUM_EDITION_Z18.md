## Corrections to paper G10 (Z-107)

1. False as worded: "a norm-preserving digraph of degree two in which two distinct vertices have a common successor needs -1 among its gains". The paper's own example B_4 has the gains 1, i, -i. Corrected: -1 lies in the group generated by the gains.
2. Overstated: "with two inputs only the conclusion is false for d >= 3". Shown for d = q = 3 only, and false for a real Hadamard block of degree four. Corrected to "can fail, as it does for d = q = 3".
3. The abstract, the introduction and "Assumptions and limits" still listed the modulus and the sum as assumed and a classification outside that form as not obtained, after Theorems 5.16 and 5.18. Rewritten. Summary sentences now carry the hypotheses of the statements they cite: degree two and abundance for Theorem 5.5; the quotient hypothesis for Theorem 5.8(ii), with "real combinations" for "mixtures"; switching invariance, no loops and constant out-degree for Proposition 4.1; disjoint successor sets and abundance for Proposition 5.10 and Theorem 5.11; "consistent" and "vanishing at zero" in two sentences on Corollaries 5.17 and 5.20.
4. Corollary 5.9: part (a) restates the hypothesis of Theorem 5.8(ii); part (d) now assumes it as well as that of Theorem 5.5 (a narrower statement than before; whether one block suffices, as a reader believes, is not examined).
5. A number without a program: the ratios 1.5, 2.5 and 4 on the complete graph on four vertices were cited to the program collection, where no program computed them. A program now does (`verify_k4_additive_ratios.py`, exact, 3 of 3 predicates), and the sentence states the weights (a = 1, F_c(1) = 2, F_c(omega) = F_c(omega^-1) = 1/2); with the other reading of "weights 2 and 1/2" the ratios are 2.5, 3 and 4.
6. Terms: "record-sum form" is defined where the form is introduced; the set U of the multiset subsection is renamed, since U is also the step; sigma_j on multisets is related to the automorphism sigma_j; V is defined in the statement of Theorem 5.16; GH-1 and GH-2 are explained; two cross-references corrected (the tree example stands after Theorem 5.3; the five-sheet instance).
7. Stale "not treated" sentences after Proposition 5.10 and Theorem 5.11 now point to the later statements. The sentence on staircases of orders 3 and 6 no longer claims a packet displacement that was computed for order 4 only. The list of known components and the bibliography entry for computations are completed.

No statement, proof or number of the paper changed apart from items 1, 2, 4 and 5.
