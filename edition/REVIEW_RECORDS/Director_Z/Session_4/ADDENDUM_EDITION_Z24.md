## Assessment Z4-X (FOCUSED): what the return requirement is, and what the checks miss

**Correction to edition Z23.** The paragraph after Corollary 5.29 of paper G10 said that near-return of the return weight "is a premise about the system". It is not. The return weight is computed with the rule's own weights, so by part (3) of the corollary the requirement is equivalent to a = 0: a condition on the rule. The stipulated candidate Z1, which used it as an independent premise, was withdrawn on 13 November for this reason (record in `candidate_Z1_third_attack/`). The paragraph, the abstract and the note on Corollary 5.28 are corrected. No labelled statement changes; the paper has 32 labelled statements and 48 pages.
