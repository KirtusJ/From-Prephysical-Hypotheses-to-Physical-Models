### DG4-02. Stronger-than-assumed additive impossibility: refuted in its broad form

**Consequence:** Gauge invariance, additivity and relational data can coexist with order-one transport sensitivity if closure information is admissible. An assumption excluding that information is required before declaring quadratic interference the *lowest possible* transport-dependent rule.

### DG4-03. Tensor composition at fixed length: supported; over-all-length claim: refuted without synchronization premises
