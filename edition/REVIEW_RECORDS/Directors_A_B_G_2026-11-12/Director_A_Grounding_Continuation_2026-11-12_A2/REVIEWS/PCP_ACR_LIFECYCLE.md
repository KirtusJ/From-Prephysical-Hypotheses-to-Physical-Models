## Small process experiment, strictly descriptive

A three-configuration sequence was used: (i) source-first acceptance criterion, (ii) strongest coherent mathematical construction, (iii) scoped contrary operational example. A useful effect was an early exact failure of sufficiency: the very same cycle contrast algebra admits a classical simplex. The sequence also prevented treating conjugate representations as necessarily physically distinct. This is **one descriptive case**, not a randomized comparison and not a validated increase in ACR accuracy. The rooted review never auto-promoted repeated positive results; the source gap in Z1/G7 was retained.

## Review recursion policy and recheck triggers

Recursion was limited to bounded mathematical or source claims, with a root-to-leaf depth of at most two focused stages. A branch closes only with proof home, inspected external support in scope, exact counterexample or explicit unresolved dependency. Root remains OPEN independently of children. Reopen affected findings when (1) full G7/Z1 source is supplied and conflicts with its quotation, (2) its assumed dimension or group action changes, (3) a valid operational discriminator breaks classical or antiunitary equivalence, (4) a numerical error certificate contradicts a trial, (5) a new source revises published classification assumptions. Propagation is by premise edge, not by all-director agreement.

No user intervention, physical experiment, change to frozen source baseline, personal data connection, or automated background job was requested or executed as part of this work.
