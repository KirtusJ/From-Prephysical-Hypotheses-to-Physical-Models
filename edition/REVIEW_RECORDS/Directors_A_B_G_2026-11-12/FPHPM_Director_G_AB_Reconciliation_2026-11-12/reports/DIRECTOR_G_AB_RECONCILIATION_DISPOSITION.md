## III. New narrow construction: separating three compositional gates

The accompanying mathematical paper proves the following conditional results in a **stipulated** complex density-matrix representation. These are not assertions that a physical qubit has emerged.

1. `DG-COMP-01`: The continuum of pair state spaces `K_t = (1-t) Sep_2 + t Quantum_2`, `0 <= t <= 1`, shares local quantum theory and is locally tomographic, convex, and stable under local CPTP operations. The usual quantum POVM effects are positive on every `K_t`. Pairwise data alone do not select a unique composite cone. **SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE**, with imported complex quantum representation explicit.
2. `DG-COMP-02`: For a Bell projector `P`, `sup_{rho in K_t} Tr(P rho)=(1+t)/2` exactly. For `rho_t=(1-t)|00><00|+t P`, the minimum partial-transpose eigenvalue is `-t/2`, and a fixed CHSH arrangement gives `sqrt(2)(1+t)`, violating the classical bound when `t > sqrt(2)-1`. **SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE**, no empirical Bell data.
3. `DG-COMP-03`: The uniform naive 4-system family `K_t^(4)=(1-t)Sep_4+t Quantum_4` is **not** closed under independent pair preparations for `0<t<1`. The exact witness gap is `Delta=t(1-t)/4>0` (and `1/16` at `t=1/2`). **REFUTED_IN_SCOPE** for the proposed naive extension, not every possible multipartite extension. Endpoints `t=0,1` remain valid controls.
4. `DG-COMP-04`: If a convex 2-system state set is already embedded in the ordinary complex density matrices, contains all pure product states and is closed under all local unitaries and an exact CNOT gate, it is the full 2-system quantum state space. Proof by single-CNOT Schmidt synthesis and convexity. **SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE as conditional theorem**, but **NOT_IDENTIFIED_IN_SCOPE** for the physical origin of CNOT and complex density operators.

The four-body theorem is the central new **adverse** check: a putative interpolation passing all local tests and any pair-level measurement may still be incompatible with parallel preparation.
