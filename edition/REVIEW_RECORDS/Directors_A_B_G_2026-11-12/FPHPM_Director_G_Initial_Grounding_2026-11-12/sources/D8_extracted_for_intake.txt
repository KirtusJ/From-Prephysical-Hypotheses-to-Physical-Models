                                            Abstract

         The entropy-difference estimate (B9) of Cross-Boundary Trace-Class Esti-
      mates for Wilson–Dirac Quasifree States uses a constant 𝑀𝑠 that bounds the
      continuum cross-block interpolants on a fractional Sobolev space. This note
      defines the relevant space, proves 𝑀𝑠 ≤ 12 + max{𝑐𝑠 , 𝑐𝑠′ } for 0 ≤ 𝑠 < 1/2, where 𝑐𝑠
      and 𝑐𝑠′ are the pointwise-multiplier norms of the indicators of the unit interval
      and of its complement on 𝐻 𝑠 (ℝ), and records that the bound is independent
      of the interpolation parameter, the scaled mass and the lattice size. The only
      external input is the classical multiplier theorem for indicators of half-lines.

Scope. The theorem supplies the constant written 𝑀𝑠 in the display preceding
(B9) of [1] and shows that it does not depend on 𝜃, 𝜇 or 𝐿. The corollary uses (B8),
the contraction property of the lattice interpolants and the polynomial coefficient
bound as stated there; it does not re-prove them, the nuclear estimate (B7), the
trace-norm comparison (B6) or the Chebyshev approximation rate. The constants
𝑐𝑠 , 𝑐𝑠′ are not evaluated numerically here. A finite-grid computation at 𝑠 = 1/4
(accompanying program check_Ms_bound.py) is consistent with the inequality; it is
a sanity check and not part of the proof.
