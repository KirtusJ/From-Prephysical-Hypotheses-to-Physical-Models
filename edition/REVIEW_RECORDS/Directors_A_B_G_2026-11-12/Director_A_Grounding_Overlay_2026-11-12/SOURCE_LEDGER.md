## Provenance labels for cross-director relay

- `BASELINE_ORIGINAL`: supplied charter/standards/registry or directly inspected baseline paper.
- `Z_QUOTE_ONLY`: a claim repeated in the human-provided Director Z disposition; no G7 text or Z1 source bytes.
- `INTERNAL_CALCULATION`: a new derivation/computation by this one AI assistant.
- `EXTERNAL_INSPECTED`: a directly read external published theorem/section.
- `EXTERNAL_EXTRACT_ONLY`: publisher/search extract or abstract rather than full audited proof.
- `INTERNAL_SELF_CHECK`: re-evaluation by the same AI; **not** independent evidence.

Do not aggregate these labels into a synthetic independent consensus. A cross-director handoff must preserve the exact source and assumptions, especially the word **homogeneous** in Gelander's theorem and the presence of **additional assumptions** in interference reconstruction results.
