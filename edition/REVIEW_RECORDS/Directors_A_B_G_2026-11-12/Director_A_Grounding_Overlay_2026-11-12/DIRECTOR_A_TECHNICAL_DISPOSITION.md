## Transport bridge: source-to-target accounting

**What is gained:** a proved relation among geometric objects **after** a complex projective probability geometry is specified; an exact sign obstruction; a large fixed-marginal family of phase alternatives; an inexpensive local metric discriminator; a precise limit-class no-go and escape control.

**What is not gained:** identification of a prephysical set of configurations, a canonical physical measure, a distinguished complex structure, a nonstipulated Born rule, operationally measurable phase interventions, a tensor-product rule, continuous reversible transformations, a state-update/measurement instrument, energy or calibrated time, a Hamiltonian, a classical limit, spacetime, or a likelihood connecting mathematical records to observations.

**Monodromy, holonomy, parallel transport and Schild's ladder** remain useful testing instruments. Their mathematical availability does not solve the representation-selection problem: the same loops admit different group representations and physical meanings. The full signed phase is not a function of an unoriented metric alone. Numerical Schild ladder approximations need proper vector scaling and convergence analysis; a small residual in one test is not an emergent dynamics result.

**Physical method:** every asserted arrow must carry a specified domain/codomain, input primitive, proof or algorithm, calibration and error model, possible counterexample, and an operational measurement procedure. An arrow labeled `stipulated`, `imported`, `asymptotic` or `external correspondence` cannot be silently relabeled `emergent`.

## Adversarial case development, without forced construction

The strongest *mathematical* positive route discovered here is a finite nonhomogeneous metric-net approximation to a projective state space, coupled with the geometric holonomy identity. This is a useful **positive control**: it verifies that Gelander's obstruction is specific to exact finite-stage homogeneity, not all finite approximations. It is **not** a prephysical toy ontology because it assumes the sphere/metric being sought. Making that input primitive and then announcing success would be circular.

The strongest *negative* route is the anti-holomorphic isometry obstruction: even **all** pairwise transition probabilities and the full unoriented Fubini-Study distance function leave orientation-related signed phases ambiguous. Three probabilities leave a larger continuous family of possibilities in `CP^2`. The missing structure can in principle be supplied by an independently justified **operational, orientation-sensitive** experiment. Nothing in this packet generates that experiment.

A quantum-to-classical semiclassical limit is not interchangeable with (i) full dephasing into a stochastic channel, (ii) attaching a classical symplectic manifold by hypothesis, or (iii) using a formal integer update index. A valid classical-mechanics emergence claim would have to specify time calibration, a phase-space map, dynamical convergence or decoherence regime, errors with units, and stability under alternative representations. Even proving a Hamiltonian limit from a stipulated Schrödinger equation would settle only a *conditional* edge until quantum dynamics itself emerges.
