# Total-ontology search: gated dependency map (not an ontology)

**Definition of target:** explained emergence of **both** quantum mechanics and classical mechanics with a complete correspondence map. A chain containing a quantum postulate, a classical mechanics postulate, a chosen clock or a stipulation of its target's state space is not a completed physical emergence. This document is a map of **obligations and negative controls**, not a statement that they are jointly satisfiable, uniquely mandated, or proven impossible.

## Starting constraint

No candidate is selected. The program accepts finite relational, categorical, algebraic, event-structural or other mathematically explicit primitives **only as investigable hypotheses**, without prior preference for signals, noise, frequency, waves, phases, time, Hilbert spaces, qubits, physical energy or symplectic geometry. They acquire physical meaning only after additional proof and calibration. A qubit need not exist: if one is proposed, distinguish (1) a merely stipulated `C^2` system, (2) an operationally identified two-level system and (3) a derived physical qubit.

## The exact edge taxonomy

`DERIVED_IN_STIPULATED_MODEL`: mathematical statement with named imported domain and proof; does **not** count as emergence.  
`OBSTRUCTED_IN_SCOPE`: counterexample or theorem defeats an exact proposal within its stated class; may be avoided by changing that class transparently.  
`CONDITIONAL`: accepted mathematical rule with assumptions unproved for a primitive universe.  
`OPEN_PRIMITIVE_TO_OPERATION`: no source or derivation selecting physical interpretation.  
`OPEN_OPERATION_TO_PHYSICS`: no calibration of an internal mathematical quantity as a physical observation.  
`IMPORT`: structure deliberately defined as an input; using an import cannot establish its own emergence.

**Currently validated internal edge examples:**

- `CP^n + FS metric + chosen complex structure -> det tangent holonomy = normalized Bargmann phase^(n+1)` is `DERIVED_IN_STIPULATED_MODEL`. All its conditions are visible. It does not select the source `CP^n`.
- `Unoriented transition metric -> unique signed phase` is `OBSTRUCTED_IN_SCOPE`, by conjugation. `Three moduli -> unique triple phase` is separately `OBSTRUCTED_IN_SCOPE`, by a PSD Gram continuum.
- `Finite exactly homogeneous metric spaces -> CP^n GH limit` is `OBSTRUCTED_IN_SCOPE` by Gelander. `Finite nonhomogeneous points in **imported CP¹** -> CP¹ GH limit` is `DERIVED_IN_STIPULATED_MODEL`, with explicit `pi/(2N)` bound.
- `No I3 + symplectic pure state manifold -> only QT` is `OBSTRUCTED_IN_SCOPE` for unrestricted model classes; a restricted Jordan-family claim can instead be `CONDITIONAL` pending a precise verified hypothesis list.
- `Static projective geometry -> unique generator and clock` is `OBSTRUCTED_IN_SCOPE`, using two distinct isometric unitary families; `prescribed dephasing -> Hamiltonian classical mechanics` is `OBSTRUCTED_IN_SCOPE` without additional coarse-graining/limit assumptions.
- `Primitive record -> physical preparation/control/clock` remains `OPEN_PRIMITIVE_TO_OPERATION`, with no supporting experiment. The absence of discovery is **not** a universal impossibility theorem.

## Priority experiment / falsification menu

**Test G-1, orientation access:** Formalize an observer's permitted data. If only all pairwise probabilities and unoriented metric is permitted, the isometry witness defeats signed-phase selection. Enlarge the access contract by a physically justified **ordered multi-copy or cyclic** measurement, not by fiat, and determine whether its construction is independent of Born/tensor postulates. Record any response to conjugation. **No experiment constructed.**

**Test G-2, geometry limit:** Attempt a finite-primitive-derived metric with nonhomogeneous approximants; report symmetry breaking, GH limit, scale normalization and uniqueness in the limit. Compare to the imported-sphere net as a positive control. Do not assume an infinite continuum secretly through coordinates. **No primitive-derived metric yet.**

**Test G-3, class discriminator:** Define a common measurement-access contract for real, complex, quaternionic, exceptional-Jordan and classical examples, with a fixed composition rule and proper third-order interference experiment. Prove either a distinctive statistic or construct a counterexample within that exact class. A geometric commutant without accessible operational reconstruction is not a test. **Partially screened, not class-closed.**

**Test D-1, dynamics:** With the same static state metric, compare `H=0` and `H=σ_z/2`. Derive from an independent primitive a law that chooses one or neither; absent such a law, classify dynamics as nonselected. Require actual calibrated clock/energy units. **Negative control proved.**

**Test C-1, classical limit:** For a proposed unitary/measurement source, define coarse-graining, scaled action, stability, solution topology and explicit error bounds on Hamiltonian trajectories. Check whether resulting process remains reversible when claimed. Dephasing alone fails as a Hamiltonian flow. **No complete limit.**

**Test E-1, empirical identification:** Specify physical sample space of records, preparation and intervention procedures, time-stamping and instrumental uncertainty; compute a likelihood and identify a falsifying observation, rather than correlating two mathematical patterns post hoc. **No admissible empirical identification.**

## Nonindependence, outliers and agenda control

Retain original numerical residuals and minor deviations; do not promote them to evidence of physics. Do not average across copies of the same LLM-derived result or treat a relay of Director Z as independently audited. Add new candidate nodes only when a noncircular derivation or an explicit negative-control test becomes possible. In this overlay the only new mathematical construction is an **imported geometry** net; it is neither an ontological choice nor a candidate universal physical model. The highest-pressure frontier is therefore **primitive-to-operation**, not a better choice of holonomy notation.
