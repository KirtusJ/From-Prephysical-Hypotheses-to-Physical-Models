SELECTED COMPANION SOURCES

This folder contains the supplied eleven-page Scientific Assessment and Further
Results and the exact dependencies needed to reproduce its typography and the
companion calibration calculation. The assessment concerns an earlier collection
of 341 files, with 81-page Collections and a 420-page Mathematical Supplement.
Its counts, page locators, execution claims and references to accompanying
records describe that source edition. They do not describe the present volumes.

The full incoming package, including the older complete volumes, inventory,
page concordance, original manifest and other execution records, is preserved
separately. This is a selected source folder, not a copy of that entire package.
The current source selection and verification scope are described in the
top-level SOURCE_EDITION_NOTES.txt and COMPUTATIONAL_SCOPE.txt.

The assessment is retained for attribution and inspection, including its
entropy nonattainment example. Its two identified historical Wilson status
sentences have prospective corrections in the present main-volume articles.
The assessment is AI-assisted work in the same research program; it is not
independent external peer review.

The accepted twelve-page companion article appears in the present supplement
as Calibration, Identified Sets and Uniform Limits. Its component PDF is
New_Studies/Calibration_Identified_Sets_and_Uniform_Limits.pdf relative to the
package root. Exact copies of its three TeX inputs are supplied both here and
under New_Studies/Calibration_and_Uniform_Limits_Source/.

To rebuild the assessment, run XeLaTeX twice on
source/Collections_Assessment_and_Further_Results.tex from its source directory.
It includes the exact retained one-page figure one directory above. That figure
belongs to this source edition; the two current program figures are in Diagram/.
To rebuild the mathematical article, run XeLaTeX twice on
Joint_Calibration_and_Sampling.tex from either complete source directory.
DejaVu Serif, DejaVu Sans and Latin Modern Math fonts are required.

The two calibration programs and their result files are unchanged supplied
sources. The second program requires the primary program and its stored result
at the relative paths included here. evidence/Calibration_Execution_Account.json
is the supplied execution account cited by the article; it is not a new
execution account for the present preparation. A separate current exact
calculation and stored-result comparison are supplied under
Computational_Material/Joint_Calibration_Check/.

Joint_Calibration_Independent_Arithmetic.json is a retained arithmetic-check
filename. It does not assert independent research provenance. The source itself
discloses shared model and source lineage and prior exposure to the first code.

Selected_Source_Index.json records the exact bytes of the eleven selected
incoming files. The top-level FILE_MANIFEST.json also covers this folder.
