1. Scope of the current changes

All 83 article, proof and terminology components of the preceding collection
remain represented. Seventy-eight component PDFs retain their exact bytes.
Five have prospective prose corrections; their original versions are preserved
separately. The changed components are:

  From Prephysical Hypotheses to Physical Models — collected article

estimates from the later finite-part and uniform modular results under their
stated hypotheses.
Three other components now scope their remaining varying-mass questions in
light of the conditional bounded-mass additive consequence. The fixed-mass
theorems, mathematical formulas and proof premises are unchanged by these
prose edits. Existing valid qualifications about other regimes remain.

Available Markdown and TeX were edited for the collected article and the
spectral-majorant article. The fourth-moment article has an edited Markdown

exact original/derivative identities are supplied under Source_Corrections/.
They require the preserved original PDFs identified there. The correction
does not claim to reconstruct a missing complete typesetting source.

The collected article also retains the earlier navigation correction to
volume contents and article references. Original PDFs and earlier editable
files remain preserved separately; the current files are identified by the

2. Opening studies and added articles

The opening studies add joint-calibration coverage and precision results,
the weak-gain inference bounds and the conditional Wilson uniform consequence.
The information identity now explicitly uses a finite or countable message
law. The deterministic natural-operation classification explicitly requires
finite integer arity and retains the nullary case. A countably infinite-arity
counterexample explains the necessity of that condition. A complete entropy
nonattainment example separates finite supremum from existence of an optimizer.
The stronger integer-partition result and its optimizer are preserved.

Calibration, Identified Sets and Uniform Limits is included from its exact
supplied twelve-page PDF. Statistical Precision under Weak Readout Gain is
a new eight-page study with full TeX, exact finite controls and stated limits.
Its lower bound uses the displayed symmetric pair; its upper construction
requires a justified positive gain restriction. Taking a convex hull preserves
scalar diameter but fills gaps, so it changes the reported feasible shape.

The program-scope figure replaces an unexplained frequency-related working
word with 'without assigned physical frequency'. Its vector PDF and SVG are
generated by one portable source. The observation figure keeps its distinct,
conditional scope. Neither is a citation for the truth of a depicted hypothesis.
