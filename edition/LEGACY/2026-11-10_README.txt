1. Reading the collection

Begin with the Collections volume. Its opening study distinguishes questions
about foundational primitives, mathematical model construction and physical
correspondence, then examines identification from restricted observations.
A synthesis and six collected articles follow. The Mathematical Supplement
contains six opening studies, five further studies and the detailed articles,
supporting proofs and mathematical terminology.

The five further studies address abstract Gaussian capacity, stochastic
recovery, calibration uncertainty, joint calibration and uniform limits, and
statistical precision under weak readout gain. Their constructions supply
mathematical implications and counterexamples under explicit assumptions.
They do not report observations of a physical substrate.

Two separate diagrams serve distinct purposes:
  Program Questions, Models and Physical Correspondence
  Structure, Observation and Physical Identification

The first organizes parallel research approaches and their requirements for
physical interpretation. The signed-sector signal proposal is one exploratory
approach. The second connects selected model assumptions, admitted observations,
conditional inferences and prospective physical tests. Neither figure asserts
an exhaustive ontology, a physical derivation or an observed test outcome.

contains the five further studies and their typesetting dependencies.
Collections/ and Mathematical_Supplement/ contain the other article, proof
and terminology components. There are 85 such component units in all, in
addition to the opening studies.

All 83 components of the preceding collection remain represented. Seventy-eight
retain their exact PDF bytes; five have limited prose corrections described in
SOURCE_EDITING_NOTE.txt. Two further studies have been added. The inherited
article set accounts for forty original manuscripts, thirty-three supporting
proof items, six additional detailed articles and one terminology reference.
All thirty-three supporting proofs remain visible pages. Some were supplied
only as PDF excerpts; inclusion does not create missing editable source.

Editable_Articles/ and Typesetting_Source/ contain available manuscript and
typesetting sources. Related_Source_Editions/ retains selected overlapping
treatments, eighteen cited mathematical notes, and the supplied scientific
assessment with the dependencies of its accepted companion calculation.
SOURCE_EDITION_NOTES.txt distinguishes these sources from the present account.
Complete superseded input editions are preserved separately. Their earlier
open-status statements do not govern the current mathematical summaries.

Computational_Material/ contains programs, certificates and outputs.
COMPUTATIONAL_SCOPE.txt identifies current exact calculations, inherited
execution accounts and source-reported outputs. Counts of successful program
predicates are not theorem counts, independent proofs or physical sample sizes.

4. Rebuilding the volumes

The PDFs are ready to read. Assembly from component PDFs needs Python 3.10 or
later, PyMuPDF, ReportLab and DejaVu Serif fonts. It does not need TeX.
Recompiling the opening studies also needs a complete XeLaTeX installation,
DejaVu Serif, DejaVu Sans, DejaVu Sans Mono and Latin Modern Math fonts.

The preparation used Python 3.12.14, PyMuPDF 1.26.6 and ReportLab 4.4.9.
Selected inherited computations use SymPy 1.14.0 and mpmath 1.3.0. The two
current exact calculation programs use Python's standard library.
requirements.txt records these packages; individual sources retain any
additional dependencies. The program-scope figure uses matplotlib for fonts.

Recompile the two opening-study documents:
  python tools/build_studies.py

Assemble with those recompiled studies:
  python tools/assemble_volumes.py --studies-dir Recompiled_Studies --output Rebuilt_From_Source

Assemble from the supplied compiled studies:
  python tools/assemble_volumes.py --output Rebuilt_Volumes

Check an unmodified extracted packet, without executing the scientific programs:
  python tools/verify_packet.py --strict-file-set

The strict file-set option expects only the distributed files. Run it before
creating additional working directories inside the extracted packet. The check
compares source link destinations and URI actions; it does not test whether
external websites are available or whether the scientific claims are true.

Commands resolve inputs relative to the package and require a new output
directory. Use --font-dir if the system DejaVu location is unsuitable.
The assembler checks component hashes, computes pagination, creates linked
contents and bookmarks, and writes both diagrams. It does not recompile all
85 components. After editing a component, compile its available source and
update its page count and digest in VOLUME_CONTENTS.json. Some excerpts lack
editable source. Inspect rebuilt pages because fonts can change line breaks.

5. Scientific scope

Preparation for scholarly assessment does not imply independent peer review,
empirical replication, novelty or institutional endorsement. The substantive
checks concern identified statements and dependencies. They are not an
exhaustive new verification of every historical proof.

Abstract channel capacity follows after specifying a state space, stochastic
kernel, input class, cost and output access. It needs no physical clock.
Selection of these ingredients from foundational hypotheses and their
physical interpretation require additional arguments and evidence.

Population identification, confidence coverage and useful precision are
different properties. The joint calibration example has exact coverage on
its declared finite family but can return nearly every admitted target.
The weak-readout study gives a two-point confidence-diameter obstruction for
its specified symmetric Bernoulli pair, and a constructive upper bound under
a warranted positive population gain restriction. The complete joint sampling
law matters. No optimal constants or physical sample recommendation is claimed.

The Wilson articles retain their stated infinite-chain, filling and scaling
conditions. The added uniform consequence is conditional on the named analytic
input estimates and a common onset over bounded scaled masses. Its additive
error bound does not establish a relative leading law for every sequence of
vanishing masses. Fixed-mass and varying-mass statements remain distinct.

A physical application requires warranted preparations, observation access,
calibration, units and controlled evidence. The collection establishes neither
a prephysical substrate nor a unique physical ontology.
