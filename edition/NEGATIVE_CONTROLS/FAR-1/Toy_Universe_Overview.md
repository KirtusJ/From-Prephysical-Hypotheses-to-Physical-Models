# The one-page account

Imagine a miniature world built from a circular row of sites. Each site carries a finite set of possible mathematical configurations. The whole row also has a single size variable. At each **formal update index**, the configurations evolve under a chosen reversible amplitude rule; a record-making operation may store some information and suppress interference; and the size variable may increase under a separately chosen expansion rule.

Unlike a cellular automaton that stores only definite bits, this world is permitted to use complex amplitudes. Unlike a conventional quantum theory presented as an explanatory achievement, this construction **admits openly that complex amplitudes, tensor products, the probability rule, and update steps are installed as axioms**. The structure can demonstrate interference and a classical-looking probability limit, but it does not explain why nature would choose those axioms.

The model is called **FAR-1: Finite Amplitude-Record Universe**, an explicitly stipulated replacement *test specimen*, **not** a replacement selected ontology. Its purpose is to offer a complete target to attack: a small world with state variables, update rules, records, observables, a simple expansion register, and reproducible examples. If it proves sterile, FAR-1 can be discarded without losing any previously supported mathematics.

The earlier TO-0 contextual toy remains withdrawn as a total-ontology candidate. It is retained only as a negative control. FAR-1 does not repair TO-0 by explaining its missing quantum selection; instead, FAR-1 changes the task and **imports quantum-like structure to build an internally executable example**. That change must not be hidden.

# What exists *inside* this mathematical world?

There are $N$ sites arranged in a ring. Each site has two basis labels, $0$ and $1$. The complete mathematical matter state is a density operator acting on a $2^N$-dimensional complex vector space. The labels are **not claimed to be physically emergent qubits**; they are stipulated two-level variables.

The ring has a positive scale $a$. Adjacent site labels are coupled, and every site can flip under a local field. The coupling strength can vary as $1/a$. A recording rule sometimes records a basis label and sometimes remains silent. The record operation causes dephasing when its outcome is ignored. The expansion rule changes $a$ using positive terms intended to resemble radiation, matter, and a cosmological constant, with an optional toy backreaction from the matter configuration.

Five choices are therefore doing most of the explanatory work: the complex state space; the update generator; the Born-style record rule; the choice of a formal update index; and the Friedmann-inspired size update. **None of these five is derived from a prephysical noise-like or incidence-only structure.**

The model distinguishes a *mathematical ontology*, where $(a,\rho,\text{record history})$ defines what the toy world contains, from a *physical ontology*, which would need independently tested bridges to actual preparations, clocks, distances and measurements. Only the former is given here.

# Three internal demonstrations

## 1. Interference with and without records

In the single-site module, two reversible mixing operations surround an adjustable relative phase. With no intervening record, the probability of outcome $0$ is

$$p_0=\frac{1+\cos\phi}{2}.$$

If the record operation dephases the intermediate state with strength $\kappa\in[0,1]$, the same output becomes

$$p_0=\frac{1+(1-\kappa)\cos\phi}{2}.$$

| Phase | No record $\kappa=0$ | Partial record $\kappa=1/4$ | Complete record $\kappa=1$ |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| $0$ | 1.000 | 0.875 | 0.500 |
| $\pi/2$ | 0.500 | 0.500 | 0.500 |
| $\pi$ | 0.000 | 0.125 | 0.500 |

These numbers were independently recomputed with a finite density-matrix implementation. They are not measurements. Crucially, the Born probability and the mixing transformation were **put into** the model. The result is an internally correct demonstration, not quantum emergence.

## 2. A classical-looking probability transition

If recording completely destroys coherence in the chosen basis, a finite unitary transformation induces a stochastic transition on diagonal probabilities:

$$T_{ji}=|U_{ji}|^2.$$

For a two-level Hadamard mixing operation, $T$ has all entries $1/2$. For a permutation operation, the transition is deterministic. These are legitimate classical probability dynamics **within the toy**.

But this does **not** derive Hamilton's equations or Newton's law. Classical stochastic behavior and classical Hamiltonian mechanics are different targets.

## 3. A growing ring

Choose illustrative, dimensionless background parameters $(\Omega_r,\Omega_m,\Omega_\Lambda)=(0.01,0.30,0.69)$. The scalar update makes a positive scale grow. At the same time the four-site matter sector evolves and creates records.

| Formal step | Scale $a$ | Mean occupied fraction | Matter purity |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 0 | 1.0000 | 0.00000 | 1.0000 |
| 1 | 1.1000 | 0.00992 | 0.9554 |
| 2 | 1.2057 | 0.03210 | 0.8470 |
| 3 | 1.3179 | 0.06107 | 0.7061 |
| 4 | 1.4377 | 0.09323 | 0.5662 |
| 5 | 1.5658 | 0.12635 | 0.4458 |

This is a dimensionless **numerical toy trajectory**, not a fit to cosmological observations. The decreasing purity measures unconditioned dephasing, not gravitational entropy production; the update index is not derived physical time; and the expansion rule was imported.

# What would have to be true for this to matter scientifically?

A serious physical interpretation would require independently justified answers to the following questions. Why is the fundamental state space complex and finite-dimensional? Why do probabilities follow the prescribed trace rule? Why this particular locality structure and interaction law, rather than others? What fixes the expansion update and its dimensionless parameters? How are finite records physically produced and calibrated? Why is the update index related to elapsed time? What establishes Lorentz symmetry and a causal speed limit? What generates actual spatial dimension and gravitational curvature? How are empirical quantities predicted with stated uncertainties? What observations distinguish FAR-1 from its simpler rivals?

FAR-1 currently satisfies **none of these identification obligations**. Its only properly supported claims concern its own algebra and numerical implementation.

A useful model can be useful without being fundamental. Lattice and quantum cellular automata have a substantial published literature; decoherent histories clarifies when alternative histories may receive probabilities; causal-set theory and Regge calculus provide independent discrete-gravity precedents. FAR-1 is not presented as a novel substitute for them [E1-E5].

# Why the mathematics cannot be stretched into cosmology

The expanding ring is not a four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime. It has no realistic light cone, gravitational waves, black-hole horizon, Einstein constraint equation, particle spectrum, structure-formation law, or actual cosmic microwave background. The scalar $a$ is a number, not a physically calibrated metric. The optional coupling between $a$ and the matter state is **not derived from a stress-energy tensor or covariant conservation law**.

The conventional Friedmann-like terms are a *benchmark analog*. Real observational cosmology tests mutually constrained measurements, including cosmic microwave background anisotropies, baryon acoustic oscillations, galaxy clustering and distance ladders. Reviews by the Particle Data Group and evolving DESI analyses demonstrate how strongly interpretation depends on multiple independent datasets [E6-E7].

The current construction cannot decide among dark matter mechanisms, whether dark energy evolves, what caused inflation, how baryons and antimatter became imbalanced, whether there is a singularity, or what happens at a black-hole horizon. Each requires additional independent theory and evidence. Their inclusion on the map is **an obligation inventory**, not a proposed solution.
