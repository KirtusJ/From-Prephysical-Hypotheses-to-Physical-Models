1. Hypotheses on the product (PROVED): at most one arc per ordered pair and out-degree d1 d2 carry over. A strongly connected derived digraph does not: the paper's own Z8 carrier zeta[[1,1],[1,-1]] times itself has a derived digraph that is not strongly connected (COMPUTED). 2. Consistency on D1 x D2 (PROVED): Ext(S1 x S2) = Ext S1 x Ext S2, so a multiplicative rule consistent on each factor is consistent on product events. On all events of the product carrier the count is consistent, and ||v^psi||^2 is consistent if and only if psi lies in U(D1) and in U(D2): the step matrix of the product is the tensor product, (A1 (x) A2)*(A1 (x) A2) = A1*A1 (x) A2*A2, which is d1 d2 times the identity exactly when each factor is a scalar matrix, the diagonals fixing the scalars. So U(D1 x D2) = U(D1) intersected with U(D2). COMPUTED: no violation on random events of length 4 of three product carriers.
