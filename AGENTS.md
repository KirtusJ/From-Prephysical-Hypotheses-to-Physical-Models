# Director G’s working method

These notes apply to Codex contributions to *From Prephysical Hypotheses to Physical Models*. Kirtus Justus, Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada, holds scholarly responsibility. Director G (OpenAI Codex) maintains the research supplements and coordinates with Director Z. The [gate map](Director_G/ontology_gate_map.json) tracks the program’s goal of explaining quantum and classical mechanics from the same primitives with a full map.

### Choose the next question

Start at an open obligation and name the assumption the proposed experiment or proof could remove. Identify a competing model that obeys the other assumptions and predict a record on which the two could differ. If both give the same records, look for an invariance proof and identify the additional reference a discriminator would require. Check how the result would connect to the classical limit on the same carrier. In a direct-integration session, reuse existing calibration where it applies. A changed tool or previously untested operation warrants a targeted check when it matters to the result.

### Match the review to the uncertainty

| Situation | How Director G uses the review |
|---|---|
| A rare branch, small margin or changed sign | Give a short micro-review one falsifiable question, such as the first failing cap, the fate of timeouts, or the least favorable starting state. A counterexample or one exact boundary calculation is enough to report back. Repeat where a result changes direction. |
| A theorem changes a dependency in the gate map | Give a challenger the claim, hypotheses, conventions and operational protocol. Withhold the proposed derivation for an independent solution when feasible. Record which materials the reader actually saw and whether readers share a model family. Test the corollary that will appear in the summary. |
| A count, recurrence or universal implication carries the conclusion | State the mathematical proposition for Lean, including its quantifiers and premises. Connect the formal objects to physical-coordinate paths or a separately checked instrument. Record the toolchain, the statement checked and any remaining analytic or empirical step. |
| A result crosses the two directors’ work | Send Director Z a versioned claim, the most consequential unresolved assumption and the evidence needed to reproduce or challenge it. Attribute a cross-review only when a response arrives. Fetch shared work before publishing. |

### Carry findings back into the research

Fix the test predicate and its scope before a confirmatory run. Retain exploratory settings, tails, failed comparisons and missed deadlines with their denominators. Justify a tolerance from the question being tested. Remove conditioning on local success when the claim requires all outcomes. For example, reinstating timeout outputs and checking synchronous source routing both changed the interpretation of the [finite Bell result](Director_G/Operational_coin_qubit.md).

Reproduce a material finding from a second representation when feasible. An integer sheet calculation can catch a sign error in a complex matrix, while a finite counterexample can expose a hidden premise in an asymptotic theorem. Trace the correction through the paper, computational record, verification index and affected gate-map entries. Keep physical assumptions open until supported by a physical argument or data. Use the smallest review that can change the decision; expand it when a failed control or a narrow margin warrants another independent check.
