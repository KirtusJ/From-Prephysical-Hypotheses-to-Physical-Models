# Research case studies

These studies trace a decision about a scientific claim in *From Prephysical Hypotheses to Physical Models*. Each identifies the calculation or manuscript under review, the evidence a reviewer could reproduce, and what that evidence changed.

| Study | Scientific outcome |
|---|---|
| [PCP delegation and scientific review (PDF)](PCP_Delegation_and_Scientific_Review.pdf#page=19) | Z1 was withdrawn because its near-return premise already selected the answer it aimed to explain. Its coefficient-sign proof survived. On the eight-site ring, recording the midpoint changed the modeled return from 1 to 1/4. See pp. 19–21. |
| [Interrupted worker reviews](Interrupted_worker_reviews.md) | Independent replays recovered the eight-tick parity comparison. The follow-up reconstructed the inspected Z18 and Z22 addenda and matched their registered SHA-256 digests, identifying exactly which manuscript versions had been reviewed. |
