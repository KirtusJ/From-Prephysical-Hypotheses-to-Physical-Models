## 7–8 November 2026 · The Volume Inflection Model

**Physical cosmology with a prescribed geometry and stress law.** The 7 November *Stationary spatial volume in an anisotropic compactification* manuscripts and the 7–8 November Volume Inflection specifications study three expanding torus directions and a contracting circle in five-dimensional Einstein gravity. At a tuned proper time the derivative and second derivative of total spatial volume vanish while volume continues to fall. Its maximum occurs earlier. The flat constraint requires negative total energy density there. The 8 November findings retain the tuned solution as a mathematical example. A periodic scalar field with all four periods finite misses the prescribed cubic coefficient at every finite aspect ratio. At equal periods it instead gives a flat-topped maximum. No measurement identifies the proposed event in our universe. The program's prephysical primitives, quantum mechanics and clock are outside this model's derivation. [8 November specification](sources/early/volume_inflection_specification.pdf) · [Dated findings and limits](sources/early/volume_inflection_findings.pdf)

Numbered *Volume Inflection* PDF downloads have distinct digests and revisions. The 15-page specification retained here identifies its version on its title page. *Volume Inflection and Superstrings* compares named compactifications and reduction tests. Failure of those reductions leaves other string constructions untested. A string comparator and a five-reading identifiability test supply separate tests of the same benchmark. [Original versions and comparator provenance](sources/early/provenance.json)

**Rolling-dilaton comparison.** A rolling scalar replaces a frozen dilaton in the specified positive-density and inverse-fifth-power stress ansatz with fixed spectators. Matching the cubic coefficient gives a quartic coefficient of **258**, versus **270** in the benchmark. This excludes the proposed reduced realization at that event. Other sources and compactifications remain open. [Mathematical review of the stipulated compactification, §§1–2](sources/early/rolling_dilaton.pdf)

## 9 November 2026 · The Relational Core Model

**v0.1 and the revised v0.3–v0.4 drafts.** A finite relational structure is reduced to a core, unique up to isomorphism within its supplied homomorphic-equivalence class. Boolean probes ask whether a homomorphism exists from a test structure. The v0.1 verdict retains the quotient theorem, and rejects uniqueness of the input, strict product preservation and a proposed finite universal loopless target. The later specification keeps the example in which the product of two \(K_2\) cores has four vertices and its core has two. Composition on equivalence classes is a separate positive result. No physical state, time evolution or probability measure is selected by the canonical representative. [Original v0.1 verdict](sources/early/relational_v01_limits.md) · [Versioned specifications](sources/early/provenance.json)

**v0.5–v0.6.** Formal products and disjoint unions lose copy multiplicity at the quotient. Comparable nonisomorphic inputs can also be absorbed. Quantitative homomorphism counts supply a positive control that can retain multiplicity. A proposed finite-dimensional strong monoidal transfer would have to represent the idempotence of a formal copy by \(d^2=d\), leaving only dimension one for nonzero Hilbert spaces under that transfer. The dossiers retain the core as a mathematical comparator and explicitly scope the obstruction to the proposed interpretation. [v0.5 revision decision and crosswalk](sources/early/relational_v05_decision.md) · [v0.6 specification provenance](sources/early/provenance.json)

**v0.8.** The two-route trial adds complex amplitudes, a Hadamard read, Born probabilities and supplied record fragments. Visibility powers follow for the specified independent fragments. A three-record example also defeats the proposed identification of negative log-overlap with metric distance. The dated disposition leaves physical composition, the quantum state and readout rule, classical motion, clock and cosmology open. The trial does not undo the earlier obstruction. This catalog records the inspected versions only. [v0.8 scientific disposition](sources/early/relational_v08_verdict.md)
