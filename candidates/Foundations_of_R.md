## Finite models used to test the proposed bridges

| Model | What the comparisons establish |

| **T2 finite Schmidt source and qubit Gibbs alternative** | The stipulated source is uniform on 180 spectra of faithful bipartite $3\times3$ states. Replacing $p_i$ by its normalized reciprocal preserves the multiset of modular log gaps. Entropy and the modular read each give 120 classes, but distinguish different pairs. The qubit Gibbs stress test changes the source. Its entropy and unordered modular spectrum give the same 13 classes in the stated test because both depend on the magnitude of the log odds. The source contract records no physical selection. | **D3 finite chiral-edge regulator** | A two-band Chern-insulator strip with mass −1, an open transverse boundary and antiperiodic longitudinal boundary conditions tests one edge-matched fermion packet. Reversing chirality swaps the preferred modular-time sign in the tested comparison. The candidate-side defect depends on the covariance floor and turns upward with scale. The data support directional response of the packet, leaving convergence, the full inclusion of algebras and fixed total capacity untested or unresolved. [18 October model, controls and adverse scale sequence](sources/foundations/d3_chiral_strip.md) |
| **Gaussian fermion benchmark and alternatives to local reconstruction** | The 19 October gate gives an explicit pair of fermion covariances $C=I/2$ and $D=I/2+e^{-P^2}/4$ as a mathematical benchmark. Two finite-mode alternatives put the same occupation excess into different modes. Their global state-norm distances from the reference both equal 1/2, while a specified one-mode read gives 1/2 and zero. A second example compares the tracial state with an equal mixture of 00 and 11. They share the entire one-particle covariance, yet their joint occupation probabilities are 1/4 and 1/2. The two comparisons test localization and the quasi-free-state assumption separately. [Gaussian physical-selection gate](sources/foundations/physical_r_gate.md) · [Mode-location pair](sources/foundations/two_mode_locality.json) · [Joint-occupation pair](sources/foundations/two_mode_joint_read.json) |
| **Four-state generator and its two rivals** | The C16 unknown-rate branch stipulates a homogeneous generator with overall rate $s$ and contrast parameter $\delta$. Its restricted response obeys $q(2\tau)=q(\tau)^2$ for the specified transformed mean. A piecewise change in rate breaks the equality. Varying only $\delta$ across launches at fixed $s$ evades the restricted test, while the second preparation exposes the change in the small-time contrast. The calibration fixes the time ratio independently. No physical R clock or four-state dynamics was selected. [Generator models and two-time test](sources/foundations/two_time_rate_models.md) |
| **Two-copy charge reference** | The distinct C16 auxiliary-coherence branch supplies two independent reference preparations on charge levels 0 and 2. An exchange observable couples the two orderings of total charge 2 and its mean gives half the real part of the product of one coherence and the conjugate of the other. This tests relative drift under specified access to the preparations and observable. It does not establish their physical availability in R.
