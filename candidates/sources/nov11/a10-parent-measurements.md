## Target definitions that may not be silently substituted

A *mathematical qubit* is a conventional reference state/effect theory on C², with positive trace-one 2×2 density matrices, effects between zero and identity, and the stipulated Born pairing. A two-outcome test, two distinguishable preparations, or a fractional probability assignment is **not** a qubit. A physical qubit requires separately warranted preparations, transformations, measurements, compositions and uncertainty. Emergence requires derivation from independently justified weaker premises, rather than importing the quantum axioms.

A *classical Hamiltonian system* has a specified phase or Poisson space, an actual dynamical generator, a physical duration parameter and an observation relation. A finite Markov chain or static probability distribution is not a derivation of classical Hamiltonian mechanics.
