### Typed reference target

A conventional **mathematical qubit** is defined as a two-dimensional complex Hilbert system with density operators `(I+r·sigma)/2` and conventional effects `0<=E<=I` paired by the Born trace. This definition is imported as a testable target. A **physical qubit** additionally requires warranted preparations, selective measurements, uncertainty, composition and control. An **emergent physical qubit** would require a derivation from weaker prephysical commitments plus a physical-identification bridge. V12 establishes neither.

The reference three-test parent-POVM models in the V12 technical report stipulate the Bloch ball, probability theory, affine preparation mixtures, and eight joint outcome labels. They **do not** derive those prerequisites from finite event incidence or from the prephysical baseline.
