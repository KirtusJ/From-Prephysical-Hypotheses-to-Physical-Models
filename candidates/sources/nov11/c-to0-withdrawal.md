## Decision: withdraw the claim to an ontology candidate

TO-0 is a **measurement-context example**, not an independently motivated physical ontology. It has no original law selecting which contextual probability model is physically permitted, no demonstrated bridge from incidence to probability, no empirical preparation/measurement contract, no selected time or dynamics, and no spacetime or gravitational equations. Its probabilities were admitted by stipulation. It could not thereby have derived them.

The previous release already *reported* its quantum-selection gate as failed and did not claim achieved emergence. The present decision does not attribute an affirmative claim to the original author: it withdraws the **candidate designation**, not the correct finite mathematics.

There is no new replacement candidate. The user-supplied notion of abstract variability is not adopted as a primitive, and the phrase "toy ontology" is deprecated for TO-0. The appropriate description is **finite contextual-probability benchmark**.

## Three precise selection failures

### 1. Incidence without probability

`P0` specifies four mathematical contexts with labelled possible outcomes. No probabilities are entailed. `P1` adds compatible context-wise probability distributions as a **new assumption**. If the labels are called remote parties or measurements, that is imported operational semantics, not an emergence of spacetime separation or an apparatus. The general name for the purely formal overlap condition is *marginal compatibility* or *no-disturbance*; a claim about physical signalling requires more structure.

### 2. Probability compatibility without quantum selection

Under P1, the very same context cover admits four distinct tables with uniform one-party marginals: local shared-bit (CHSH 2), imported complex-quantum reference (CHSH 2 sqrt(2)), PR (CHSH 4) and uniform independent (CHSH 0). Normalization and overlap consistency are verified by full enumeration. PR has no global deterministic assignment.

- Retain only P0/P1: cannot select complex quantum mechanics or classical mechanics.
- Add a global noncontextual assignment postulate: excludes the stipulated quantum-reference violation of CHSH, so cannot be credited with deriving it.
- Add Hilbert spaces, Born probabilities or a quantum compositional rule: imports the sought structure and cannot count it as a consequence.

These alternatives establish a countermodel to **sufficiency** of P0/P1, not a disproof that some as-yet-unknown stronger theory might exist.

### 3. Static probability without dynamics

The same static law `(1/2,1/2)` is stationary under the identity and the flip stochastic transition matrices. Their adjacent-record agreement probabilities are 1 and 0. Therefore the static distribution does not uniquely identify a transition rule. The two-step index has been **introduced**, not derived as physical time. Neither process produces a Hamiltonian, symplectic form or continuum dynamics.

## Overfitting and redundant latent ontology

For every rational finite conditional table P(y|x), choose an integer D clearing denominators and a uniform latent variable U on {1,...,D}. For every input context x, partition those D labels into outcome blocks of sizes D P(y|x). A deterministic function f(x,U) reproduces the table exactly. Adding an independently sampled, never-reported latent K of arbitrary cardinality leaves the complete observations unchanged.

For a PR table, a particularly simple context-dependent realization is `a=U`, `b=U xor (x*y)` with U a uniform bit. This requires the **joint input** `(x,y)` and does not give a local hidden-variable model when those inputs represent physically separate parties. It only demonstrates the arbitrariness of unrestricted context-dependent representation.

Thus permitting arbitrary latent distinctions does not uniquely select an underlying ontology. Forbidding them or imposing minimality must be a new principled condition, not an unnoticed preference.

**Reclassification:** mathematically supported under imported reference-state, preparation, effect and sampling assumptions. This is NOT a prediction of P0/P1. It does not contradict separate 29- and 30-record results for different parent pairs. It does not prove the global optimal sample size for any wider experiment.

### Global correlation cap

The incoming bound `sup |a+b dot r|=1/(2 sqrt 3)` is supported as a single-parent mathematical theorem under the specified preparation-affine and positivity hypotheses. The exact positivity inequality is

    |a| + max_{v in T} ||b+v/2||_2 <= 1

where T consists of the four even-parity sign vectors. Their tetrahedron's inradius yields the upper bound; the specific boundary choice `a=0`, `b=(-1,-1,-1)/6`, `r=(-1,-1,-1)/sqrt(3)` attains it. It does NOT automatically bound two-parent TV separation sharply and is not quantum emergence evidence.
