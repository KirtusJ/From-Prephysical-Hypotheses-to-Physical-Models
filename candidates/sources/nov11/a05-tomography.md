## B. New demonstrated defect

For the *specific* box fiducial, the POVM is **never informationally complete** at finite `N>=2`. Its exact operator span dimension is `R_N` in the technical disposition, growing at most as `N(2sqrt(N)-1)` out of `N²`, with an inaccessible fraction tending to 1. At `N=4`, full-rank states with trace distance `1/2` are exactly indistinguishable by its entire outcome distribution.

## C. Repair does not select an ontology

Generic nearby fiducials have nonzero overlaps and can provide informationally complete Weyl-covariant POVMs. For perturbation norm `delta_N`, formerly blind Fourier coefficients have magnitude at most `2delta_N`; their recovery is ill-conditioned as `delta_N` tends to zero. Positivity, covariance, asymptotic consistency and informational completeness do not identify which fiducial is realized by nature, which outcomes are physically accessible, or which selective instrument produces them.

## D. Stronger quantitative incompatibility

For a positive unital map of the full commutative torus algebra, approximating the two exact Weyl unitaries in operator norm must incur error at least `2-sqrt(4-sin(pi/N))`, which is `Omega(1/N)`. This is an algebraic limit, not a physical noise floor.
