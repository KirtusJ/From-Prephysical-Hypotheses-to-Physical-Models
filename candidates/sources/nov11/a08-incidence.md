# Director A | Incidence-First Operational Reconstruction, V8

**STATUS: UNSELECTED, PROVISIONAL, REPLACEABLE.** The previous V7 groupoid/Weyl/POVM route has been withdrawn as a candidate ontology. The mathematical proofs from V7 are still indexed as comparison controls.

## Typed target vocabulary

- `Q-MAT`: Conventional *mathematical qubit*, `H=C^2`, `D(H)`, effects `[0,I]`, Born trace pairing, specified channels and tensor-composition rules. Stipulation, not emergence.
- `Q-OP`: Empirically tested *effective operational qubit* for a declared preparation/measurement/control/composition menu, error metric and calibration contract. Unestablished in this program.
- `Q-EM`: *Physically emergent qubit*, requiring a derivation from weaker independently warranted premises and an evidence-grounded mapping to `Q-OP`. Open.
- `C-MECH`: Hamiltonian classical mechanics, requiring phase space, Poisson/symplectic structure, selected Hamiltonian and physically calibrated time. Not equivalent to a finite Markov chain.
- `P0`: Abstract incidence/test compatibility **before** probability, time or physical observation.
- `P1`: Declared additive probability-weight bridge, not a derived physical rule.

## First exact controls

1. All normalized weights on finite tests form a polytope. Affine reductions preserve polytopes. This cannot select the exact three-ball qubit state space.
2. Three disjoint binary test menus allow the full cube of expectation triples; the quantum ball is a stricter selected subset. Both satisfy test normalization.
3. A classical 8-ontic-response model matches each of the three static binary-test marginals, but its explicit lift fails affine preparation-mixing consistency by 1/32 on one witness.
4. An affine automorphism group of a finite full-dimensional polytope is finite. It cannot supply a nontrivial continuous reversible one-parameter group without added structure/limit.
5. A stipulated full Euclidean rotation action selects a ball, but the metric, dimension and symmetry group have been added as strong premises; a qubit and physical quantum mechanics are not thereby derived.

## Research gates, all open

**G1. Event/physical identification.** Are the formal incidence labels experimentally operational, or merely mathematical? A physical bridge must specify what constitutes a test/outcome.

**G2. Probability selection.** Why should compatible event weights be additive, and why should the physically realizable subset be the ball rather than the cube or another convex body?

**G3. Nonclassicality discrimination.** Supply observations and operations that rule out a classical or other GPT comparator on the entire declared test menu, not only reproductions of selected marginals.

**G4. Continuous transformations.** Derive or independently warrant a continuous reversible group; the finite test-automorphism group is insufficient.

**G5. Quantum composition.** Recover the physically justified system boundaries, tensor/alternative composition rule, instrument effects and multi-system statistics without importing them.

**G6. Emergent classical mechanics.** Derive phase space, Poisson/symplectic structure, Hamiltonian flow, measurement bridge and physical clock, with a controlled limit. Records and Markov chains alone do not suffice.

**G7. Comparative elimination.** Attempt to refute the candidate, document surviving classical/nonquantum models, evaluate assumption costs against Hardy/Chiribella/Plávala reconstructions, and withdraw if no differential prediction/derivation is obtained.

None of G1–G7 is complete. The stages are bookkeeping of the **provisional construction**, not a promotion of the parent PCP beyond Initial External Grounding.
