Parent Unary PCP: Initial External Grounding OPEN. Candidate has NO physical acceptance or steelman-stage authorization.

## Reader warning

**Give this mathematical toy almost no evidential weight as an ontology.** Its primitive context cover overlaps well-established empirical-model and sheaf contextuality frameworks. It does not produce a physical quantum field, classical Hamiltonian, physical clock, spacetime or cosmology. It is retained only to test explicit selection rules and failure modes. The user's metaphor of abstract 'noise distinctions' is *not* included as a literal material, frequency, wave or necessary primitive.

## TO-0 primitive and its optional bridges

- `P0` (**stipulated**, no probability): finite measurement-context index set `C`, outcome-label sets `O(C)`, and restriction maps `r_DC` with compatible compositions. Even the word 'measurement' here is a mathematical label, not an empirical fact. No time or spatial dimension is present.
- `P1` (**not derived**): for each context assign `p_C ∈ Delta(O(C))` with overlapping-marginal compatibility. This adds probability as a new primitive. Its operational version is a no-disturbance/nonsignalling consistency condition; it does not select a physical model.
- `P2` (**optional, not derived**): demand a global noncontextual joint law on all labels, restricting the toy to a local classical arm.
- `P3` (**optional, not derived**): choose known complex quantum state/effect/tensor rules, giving a quantum comparator. THIS IS AN IMPORTED REFERENCE THEORY, NOT AN EMERGENCE RESULT.
- `P4` (**optional, not derived**): admit any compatible nonsignalling empirical table, including postquantum examples.
- `P5` (**open**): introduce physically testable preparation, selective record, measurement calibration and transformation contracts; none is supplied by P0-P4.
- `P6` (**open**): independently justify time, differentiable dynamics, phase space or symplectic geometry; none follows from the context labels.
- `P7` (**open**): independently justify spacetime metric, gravity and cosmological predictions; none follows from a directed incidence graph.

This is a deliberately *nullable* candidate. If its only explanatory content comes from P1-P7 being imported, the claimed total ontology is to be **withdrawn**, while its finite examples may remain useful.

## Toy contexts and three genuinely different probabilistic models

Choose `x,y ∈ {0,1}` and recorded `a,b ∈ {0,1}`. The four maximal contexts are `C_xy={A_x,B_y}`. Restriction maps discard the other party's label. Under the optional probability bridge P1, local consistency is nonsignalling: summing over `b` cannot depend on `y` and vice versa.

1. Local shared-bit arm: sample `lambda` uniformly from `{0,1}`, and set `a=lambda`, `b=lambda` in every context. All `E_xy=1`, so `CHSH=2`. It has a global hidden assignment.
2. Quantum-reference arm: **stipulate** `|Phi+>`, Pauli `X,Z`, Bob axes `(Z+X)/sqrt(2)` and `(Z-X)/sqrt(2)`. The correlators are `E00=E01=E10=1/sqrt(2)` and `E11=−1/sqrt(2)`, so `CHSH=2sqrt(2)`. This imports Hilbert space and Born's rule, so it cannot be called emergent.
3. PR comparator: put probability `1/2` on the two outcomes satisfying `a XOR b = x*y`, and zero on the others. It is nonsignalling, gives `CHSH=4`, and cannot have a global deterministic assignment: the XOR of the first three context constraints conflicts with the fourth.
4. Uniform comparator: assign probability `1/4` to each outcome in each context, giving `CHSH=0`.

All four have uniform single-party marginals, so single-party observations alone do not identify the arm. Even nonsignalling is insufficient to select the quantum arm, since it admits PR. This is a *positive finite mathematics* example of **negative physical-model selection**.

## Extra toy controls

- Triangle of binary pair tests: `p_a+p_b=p_b+p_c=p_c+p_a=1` has the unique normalized solution `p_a=p_b=p_c=1/2`, but NO deterministic 0/1 valuation. Absence of a deterministic valuation is not enough to select quantum physics; a singleton state body is not a Bloch ball.
- Dynamic nonselection: the uniform binary state `(1/2,1/2)` is stationary under BOTH the identity and flip transition matrices. A formal two-step record gives same-label probability 1 versus 0. The common static law does not select dynamics. The step index is NOT an independently justified physical clock.
- Higher-order parent ambiguity: the accompanying exact 28-record control stipulates an entire Euclidean reference ball and affine probability effects. Even with those added assumptions, all proper marginals need not select the complete parent instrument.

## Precise rejection gates

`G-S`: Explain why complex quantum is selected over local and PR arms without assuming the complex quantum model. **FAIL.**

`G-P`: Derive probability, mixture affinity and any source independence from P0, rather than stipulate them. **OPEN.**

`G-Q`: Derive a qubit only IF a qubit actually emerges; no qubit is required. **OPEN / optional outcome.**

`G-T`: Derive and independently calibrate physical time and reversible/Hamiltonian flow, beyond arbitrary step labels or stochastic kernels. **OPEN.**

`G-X`: Derive geometry, dimension, Lorentzian causal structure, gravity and quantitative predictions against independently observed data. **OPEN.**

`G-O`: Specify observed preparations, transformations, selective records, calibrated instruments and state/effect uncertainty, with a falsifiable discriminator against competitors. **OPEN.**

`G-F`: Limit free parametrization and validate on data not used to tune the candidate. **FAIL for unconstrained empirical-table encoding.**

## Cosmology, pure mathematics and constants

No Friedmann equations, Einstein equations, quantum field equations, CMB spectrum, galaxy formation history, dark matter or dark-energy dynamics have been derived. Cosmological implications remain **not constructed**, not merely untested.

The Riemann hypothesis, P vs NP, Hodge conjecture, Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture, Yang–Mills existence/mass gap, and Navier–Stokes Millennium formulation have no supplied proof or counterexample from TO-0. The Poincare conjecture was already solved. As of 11 November 2026 the Clay Institute still labels Navier–Stokes Active while evaluating an announced apparent solution. A numerical or physical analogy is not a proof.

If ordinary real mathematics is explicitly adopted, define the mathematical `pi = 4 arctan(1)` and `tau_turn=2*pi`. These are conventions; **no physical circle or duration has been obtained**. `tau_turn` is distinct from inversion-bias symbols in older detector studies.
