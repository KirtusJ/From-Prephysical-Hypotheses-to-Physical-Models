## What would have to emerge?

A *mathematical qubit* means the conventional complex two-dimensional Hilbert-space reference theory with the Bloch-ball state set, positive effects, Born trace pairing, declared channels and declared tensor composition. An *operationally identified qubit* additionally requires independent empirical evidence for a named preparation/effect/control/composition menu within quantified error and calibration bounds. A *physically emergent qubit* further requires a derivation of the operational structure from independently grounded, weaker premises. None has been established here.

*Classical mechanics* means more than records or a Markov chain: a physical phase space, Poisson or symplectic structure, selected Hamiltonian, physical dynamical parameter, observation law, and controlled trajectory limit where claimed.

## Stages and non-importation rule

- **P0: abstract incidence/refinement.** Formal event labels and hyperedges only. A hyperedge is not a physically performable apparatus merely because called a 'test'. No time, frequency, Hilbert space, complex amplitude, spatial geometry or probability.
- **P1: optional operational-probability bridge.** Explicitly stipulate additivity, normalization, preparation mixing and identification of physical records *only if independently warranted*. The maximal finite incidence model is polyhedral.
- **P2: competing state bodies.** Do not privilege the Bloch ball. Test classical simplex/cube, polygon/GPT alternatives, real disk, complex ball, and other mathematically coherent choices against a declared principle and accessible observations.
- **P3: test compatibility and operational mixture preservation.** Separate pointwise marginal representations from affine preparation models. The new joint-response theorem gives a sharp visibility bound of \(1/\sqrt d\) for \(d\) orthogonal binary reference tests on a stipulated Euclidean unit ball.
- **P4: composites.** Ask whether local tomography, tensor structure, subsystem distinction, and any entanglement are derived from lesser assumptions or imported. An abstract \(M_4(\mathbb C)\) does not select its factorization.
- **P5: records and instruments.** Require selective outcome labels and externally calibrated effects; an unread channel cannot identify a particular instrument or definite record.
- **P6: dynamics and time.** Require an actual selection criterion for generator/flow, continuous or limiting structure, and calibrated physical time. No nontrivial \(\mathbb R\)-action is possible on the unchanged finite full polytope through its affine automorphism group.
- **P7: classical mechanical limit.** Identify phase space and Poisson algebra, a controlled Hamiltonian/trajectory limit, mass/action parameters and measurement mapping; a stochastic transition is insufficient.
- **P8: independent empirical discrimination and withdrawal.** Require observations that distinguish candidate models, a documented calibration/uncertainty chain and independently adjudicated model checking. If a purported emergence theorem relies on quantum ingredients inserted as premises, withdraw the *emergence* claim even if the construction remains mathematically valid.

## Immediate positive and negative controls

**Negative:** finite linear incidence constraints produce a polytope, not the exact three-dimensional Bloch ball. Its affine reversible automorphism group is finite, so even an algebraic one-parameter action of \((\mathbb R,+)\) is trivial. Three independent binary-test normalization laws allow a cube corner excluded by qubit positivity.

**Positive with explicit imports:** a jointly distributed deterministic classical assignment can reproduce all \(d=2,3\) *unsharp* conventional Pauli marginals while preserving mixtures whenever the common visibility is at most \(1/\sqrt d\). The exact construction is in the mathematical dossier. The quantum Bloch ball and its corresponding effects are reference premises, not ontological results.

**Negative selection control:** the earlier pointwise hidden-variable formula reproduces separate sharp marginals but violates affinity under preparation mixing. It is not a full operational simulation.

**No-go limitation:** the above does not show quantum theory uniquely selected, nor that the required physical events or measurements are accessible, nor that finite-data polytope models are the only alternatives.
