## Current decision

The previous groupoid/Weyl/POVM route is **withdrawn as a candidate ontology**. Its valid finite mathematical results are retained as comparators. No total quantum/classical emergence theorem is claimed.

## Typed reference targets

1. **Mathematical complex qubit (external reference):** density operators on C² and positive effects; states parametrized by Bloch vectors r∈B³. This and the Born trace formula are *supplied*, never counted as emergent.
2. **Operational qubit (unestablished):** reproducible, independently calibrated preparation/effect/transform statistics fitting the full chosen single- and composite-system reference theory with explicit uncertainty.
3. **Emergent physical qubit (unestablished):** derivation and independent physical identification from weaker prephysical assumptions, not only a representation of imported qubit algebra.
4. **Classical Hamiltonian mechanics (unestablished):** selected phase space with symplectic or Poisson structure, evolution generator and physically grounded temporal parameter. A finite Markov chain does not meet this target.

## Selection gates, all currently open

- **Probability bridge:** independently justified observational probability rather than normalized formal weights alone.
- **Feasibility gate:** nonempty normalized operational state space; reject inconsistent event/test systems.
- **State-body selection:** determine a nonpolyhedral law from warranted structure, without assuming an entire Bloch ball or continuum.
- **Full observation law:** explain how three-way (or higher) correlations, unobserved from proper marginals, are selected or bounded.
- **Preparation selection:** explain which mathematical reference preparations are operationally accessible and how their probabilities are calibrated. V11 gives sharp 149-versus-45 example of why this matters.
- **Classical-comparator audit:** preserve the octahedral, cube, general GPT, continuous classical latent, and trivial representation alternatives. The same partial marginals cannot be taken as uniquely quantum evidence.
- **Transformation/composition/clock bridges:** justify selective instruments, composition, continuous dynamics and physical time separately.

## Research eliminations and cautions

- No special ontological preference for incidence structures or for any proposed selection principle.
- No silently imported complex amplitudes, Born rule, quantum tensor products, Hamiltonians, Euclidean geometry, physical clocks, or noncontextuality assumptions.
- New V11 positive parent distributions and sample counts are conventional quantum-reference *mathematics*, not physical apparatus demonstrations.
- The sharp center-bound permits a different distinguishability threshold at a different preparation; population nonidentification and finite-sample weakness must not be confused.
- If a candidate only reproduces known GPT or qubit mathematics through explicitly imposed geometry and observation laws, it will be retained as a reformulation/comparator, **not** promoted to physical emergence.
