## Decision, recorded without retroactive editing

**Withdraw** the proposed sequence `bare pair groupoid → physically selected M_N(C) → emergent quantum and classical mechanics` as an ontological derivation. The arrows still model matrix units, and the V2 fixed-mode Weyl limit still works mathematically. But the crucial *selection* and *operational identification* claims are not established, with explicit countermodels now available. Do not patch the candidate by adding unearned laws for every missing bridge merely to retain the conclusion.

**Retain** the construction as a set of transparent conditional positive and negative controls. If future evidence supplies a primitive that distinguishes groupoid presentations or identifies a physical observation algebra, that new evidence may reopen this track. Its source and checking criteria must be independently stated first.

## Minimal V3 construction under consideration (not an ontology)

Define a **bridge-selection problem** whose input is a candidate mathematical relational structure and an explicitly declared equivalence relation on presentations, and whose output is an operational theory only when independently warranted data are supplied. This is a formal audit architecture, not a claim about what exists.

A candidate must provide all of:

1. **Presentation invariance:** declare whether isomorphism, equivalence of categories, Morita equivalence, or a weaker notion represents physically irrelevant description changes. Test that predicted observables and records are invariant in the declared sense. Do not assume object multiplicities are physically meaningful.
2. **Quantum primitives without circular emergence:** state precisely which scalars, involution, state cones, effects and composite/tensor rules are supplied. Demonstrate that the stated primitives actually select complex quantum mechanics over classical, real or other generalized probabilistic models, or record nonidentification.
3. **Instrument versus channel:** specify the accessible record port and its conditional statistics. Unconditioned state suppression alone is not a definite outcome law. Require counter-model tests against random-unitary realizations.
4. **Subsystem and gauge:** specify physically meaningful local subalgebras and a permissible gauge group. The abstract `M_4(C)` alone does not select a unique entanglement factorization.
5. **Classical sector:** distinguish unitary+pinching bistochastic chains, general CPTP realizations of Markov processes, and reversible Hamiltonian mechanics. Record additional environmental and selection resources.
6. **Dynamics and physical time:** select a one-parameter law on its own merits and provide an independent bridge from auxiliary mathematical parameters or iteration counts to measured time. The user has not stipulated any prephysical physical-frequency carrier.
7. **Geometry and constants:** if torus/Poisson, spatial metric, action scale or Planck parameter is introduced, label it as imported until selected by independent premises or observations. Include controlled high-frequency and continuum uniformity tests.
8. **Falsification and competitors:** for each claimed emergent structure, construct an equally observation-compatible alternative without it when possible. Preserve those negative findings with full force; do not equate failure to identify with proof of nonexistence.

## Resume or replacement trigger

Do not reopen the pair-groupoid **as a physical origin** merely by proving another matrix identity. First supply (i) a justified presentation-equivalence rule; (ii) one operational discriminator excluding at least one major rival such as a real/classical/identity-only model; (iii) access to conditional records; and (iv) a credible physical scale/time or dynamics identification path. Every added premise must be counted as an import, and external experimental provenance must not be inferred from synthetic statistics.

**Absent such evidence, a negative scientific outcome is acceptable:** the construction is ordinary finite quantum-compatible mathematics with useful falsification tests, not a foundation of quantum plus classical mechanics. The mathematical results still remain valid.
