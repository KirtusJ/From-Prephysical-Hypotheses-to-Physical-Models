# Physical premises and their evidence obligations

This is a research evidence ledger, not a claim-classification registry. Physical-model selection remains **UNSELECTED**. A mathematical theorem establishes a consequence conditional on its inputs; it does not certify that an apparatus meets those inputs.

| Input to the theorem | Present mathematical result | Evidence still needed for a physical use |
|---|---|---|
| Fermionic modes and occupation effects | CAR identities and a finite quadratic generator are explicitly specified. | Identify the actual modes and verify that the effective statistics, interactions, leakage and measurement effects satisfy the stated model. A bosonic one-particle analogy does not alone supply the filled fermionic state. |
| Initial preparation | Earlier finite-ground-state results and conservative fidelity certificates are retained separately. | Certify the selected actual ensemble with its preparation/transfer error. An infinite-ground-state marginal and a finite ground state are different preparations. |
| First instrument | Correct effects plus perfect repeatability can erase the clock signal. A new theorem instead uses post-instrument J² and leakage. | Measure those quantities after the actual first instrument, including its complete backaction or reset. |
| Separate prepare-and-read branch | The same theorem applies to a calibrated prepared state and a single terminal occupation effect, with exposure one. | Certify initial leakage and J², transfer, evolution, time and terminal readout. The protocol's zero reference label is not a measured first occupation. |
| Post-instrument coefficient | J² is the indicator of exactly one fermion in the two orthogonal modes f,v. | Implement and calibrate the required mode conversion and terminal parity/occupation diagnostic; retain zero/two-particle, loss and erasure events. |
| Label support | Leakage is disagreement between the recorded first label and an immediate target-occupation diagnostic. | Independently bound the diagnostic effect and any evolution during calibration. Repeating the same uncharacterized readout is insufficient. |
| Final measurement | The proof uses target occupation effects; separately bounded error can enter directional budgets. | Bound the event-probability defect on the relevant ensemble, or establish a stronger justified channel bound. Average assignment fidelity is not automatically a diamond bound. |
| Generator | One-particle operator-norm error controls evolved occupation projections without a particle-number factor. | Calibrate couplings and phases and bound unwanted interactions, leakage and drift throughout the observation window. Population fits alone do not establish the full operator bound. |
| Time | The law depends on gamma times physical elapsed time. | Establish the clock conversion and uncertainty, including pulse endpoints, finite ramps and detection latency. |
| Length | A calibrated spacing permits q=k/a and velocity scale v=gamma a. | Establish the spatial referent and length calibration. A momentum-space index lattice is not automatically physical position. |
| Fixed-sample concentration | The all-count KL endpoint covers the mean of conditionally independent heterogeneous Bernoulli flags. | Establish the required sampling/conditioning design for the actual records. |
| Adaptive/continuous monitoring | The new confidence sequence uses conditional means or simultaneous cumulative lower envelopes. | Establish calibration-to-science transfer for every prefix under the original observation law. A single terminal average certificate is insufficient. |
| Precision near zero | Root-n is available under specified shrinking errors; exact-zero monitoring avoids a log-log penalty. | Control both retained channels, background, leakage and systematic floors. A finite zero-event calibration does not prove exact zero leakage. |
| Continuum interpretation | A low-momentum symbol limit and an exact fixed-volume spectral limit are proved. | Demonstrate the physical scaling family and decide which observables and states converge. No full field-theory, spacetime or universal causal-structure theorem follows here. |

## Source use

Primary papers in `proofs/instrument_bridge.txt` establish individual control, imaging or instrument-characterization ingredients at their stated scope. The register identifies full-PDF passages versus abstract-only access. The statistical primary sources in `proofs/anytime_inference.txt` establish general test-supermartingale methods; the displayed finite-device specialization is derived in the packet. The metrological definitions linked in `proofs/multiscale.md` support explicit calibration and uncertainty requirements. None supplies measurements on the proposed combined apparatus.

## Outstanding decisions

Select an operational target only after its preparation, instrument, evolution and calibration can be supported together. Decide whether the intended result is a one-sided scale upper bound, an exact-ground-state joint mass/scale inverse, or a continuum observable theorem; these require different premises. Preserve that distinction in any future Paper, Methodology and Glossary revisions.
