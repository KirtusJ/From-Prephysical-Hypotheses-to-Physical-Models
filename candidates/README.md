## Adding or retiring a model

When a director, subagent, hosted model or nested worker stipulates a candidate, record it here at the first handoff, including proposals abandoned before a branch or paper is issued. A worker without repository access sends the primary text or patch and its disposition to its parent. The integrating director verifies the source and adds the entry. Include the originator’s role and the repository, revision, file, or archive member that fixes the version. Give each independent formulation its own entry. For a revision or internal model, name its parent, the changed premise and its disposition. Record the originating director or worker, source version and date, an auditable specification, the decisive test or open question, and any results that survive. When an attack ends a candidate, append the dated decision and link the failed claim, witness and original files. Preserve the earlier version and failed, null or outlying results. Later use of one lemma or of the model as a control does not reopen the original proposal. [Candidate review process](../Director_Z/candidate/CANDIDATE_PROCESS.md)
