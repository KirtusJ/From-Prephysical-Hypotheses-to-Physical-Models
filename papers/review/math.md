## Coverage limits

The corpus inventory includes all issued A–G volumes. The mathematical checks above sample D5–D8, F6 and G1–G10, and reconcile the stated scope of D3 and D4. Unlisted theorem proofs, underlying empirical sources, the full concentration-connection and boundary trace arguments, historical superseded editions, and the separate working-note and candidate collections still require their own review. No test here measures or identifies an emergent physical law.
