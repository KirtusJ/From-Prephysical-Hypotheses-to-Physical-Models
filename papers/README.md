# Research papers

Kirtus Justus, Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada, holds scholarly responsibility for the papers. The [contribution record](../CONTRIBUTORS.md) identifies the work of ChatGPT, Codex and Claude.

Open a title for the complete PDF. The seven series contain 40 papers. F7 is listed separately as a plan. The source column opens the editable material available for each paper or its parts. Some parts survive only as PDFs.

[Mathematical review and corrections](review/math.md) · [Checks across series](review/independent_checks.md) · [PDF review](review/pdf.md) · [Machine-readable catalog with SHA-256 hashes](catalog.csv)

The [issued edition](../edition/README.md) preserves the original pagination used in its [theorem register](../edition/PROGRAM/registers/THEOREM_REGISTRY.csv). For G7–G10, cite the numbered statement or section and identify the reading copy. ## Verification

Run `python tools/verify_papers.py` from a checkout with PyMuPDF installed. The catalog records the checksum and pagination of each reading copy alongside the corresponding issued PDF. The mathematical and PDF reviews give the scope of further checks.

The revised G7–G10 sources use TeX Gyre Pagella and Latin Modern Math. To typeset one of them with Tectonic 0.17, enter its source directory and run, for example, `tectonic G7_manuscript.tex`. The [math-text repair script](review/restore_math_text.py) and [PDF review](review/pdf.md) document the seven reading copies with repaired character mappings.
