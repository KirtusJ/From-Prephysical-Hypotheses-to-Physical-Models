## Iris and the atlas proposals

Iris has its own revision history. Between the withdrawn metric mechanism and the three-wall model, a preliminary Iris diagram specified two wall sectors and a material-probe crossover within an assumed four-dimensional background. The diagram was inspected separately from the retained ZIP (`Iris_F_Final_Preliminary_Model(1).svg`, SHA-256 `ac6d1d7289195bfef203addd4c93bd5ef7e760fc2fbc35045b3e75f48f1d1811`). Section 1 of the retained refinement identifies the inherited action and the added diagonal wall. The other atlas entries below retain their own authors’ identifiers.
