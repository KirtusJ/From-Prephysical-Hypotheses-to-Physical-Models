# Fiction and mixed-source quarantine

**Iris (F) is fiction.** Its wall calculations and scattering data describe a stipulated universe. The synthetic output belongs with that specification, outside the research evidence base. [Iris specification and source notice](fiction/README.md#iris-and-the-atlas-proposals)

| Collection | What belongs here |
| --- | --- |
| [Fiction archive](fiction/README.md) | Invented cosmologies, their simulated outputs and attacks on their internal assumptions. Includes Iris, the atlas proposals, Counterseal and the fictional precursor to Volume Inflection. |
| [Mixed historical packets](mixed/README.md) | Original documents that combine or discuss the fictional proposals alongside conditional graph, detector or reconstruction mathematics. Each packet is preserved whole with its original digest. |
| [Research candidate catalog](../candidates/README.md) | Scholarly proposals, conditional mathematical models and explicitly constructed negative controls. Each has its own assumptions, evidence and disposition. |

Material in the two quarantine collections is available for archival reading. Exclude it from research evidence searches, verification indexes and ontology gate decisions. A mathematical claim first encountered in a mixed packet can enter research through a separately stated result with explicit assumptions, an independently checked derivation or replay, and research provenance. Report a synthetic result as a model output whenever it is discussed.

The original [combined index at the pre-separation revision](https://github.com/KirtusJ/From-Prephysical-Hypotheses-to-Physical-Models/blob/e0bca64afa6919e36dbc63bc13c0e25c3aad1e5c/candidates/sources/early/provenance.json) records the provenance before the split. Run `python quarantine/check_boundary.py` from the repository root to verify retained files against the three current manifests.
